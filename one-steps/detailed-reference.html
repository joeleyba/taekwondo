2. White Belt & Yellow Belt Technical Breakdown

Rising Block & Reverse Punch (White Belt #1)

Page Citation: Master Tom Malone's Notes (p. 45)

Biomechanics: Step back 45° with right foot into Left Walking Stance (*Gunnun Sogi*). Execute Left Rising Block (*Bakkat Palmok Nopunde Makki*) crossing forearms at chest level before driving upward at 45° angle. Execute Right Middle Reverse Punch (*Bandae Jirugi*) targeting solar plexus.

Inside Horse Stance "Pizza Man" Double Strike (Yellow Belt #2)

Page Citation: Master Tom Malone's Notes (p. 68)

Biomechanics: Step forward inside opponent's lead punch into Sitting Stance (*Annun Sogi* / *Juchum-Seogi*). Simultaneously execute Left High Knifehand Block (*Sonnal High Makki*) and Right Neck Knifehand Strike (*Sonnal Mok Taerigi*) in a dual-palm extension.

3. 1st Dan Hapkido Joint Locks & Positional Defenses

Achilles Tendon Leg Bar Defense

Page Citation: Master Tom Malone's Notes (p. 698-699)

Biomechanics: From right kneeling posture, palm heel strike right inner knee while sweeping ankle with left hand. Wrap right arm around lower calf, applying small-circle wrist extension against Achilles tendon.

Shoulder Throw against Two-Hand Choke

Page Citation: Master Tom Malone's Notes (p. 708)

Biomechanics: Grab right wrist with left hand. Swing right arm over opponent's left elbow, placing crook of right elbow beneath armpit. Squat low into Horse Stance, pivot hips 180°, and execute shoulder throw over right hip.
