1. General Choi Hong Hi Encyclopedia (Volume 5 Verified Page Ranges)

General Choi Hong Hi's 15-Volume Encyclopedia of Taekwon-Do (1985 Edition / 1999 Condensed Reference), Volume 5, organizes prearranged sparring and self-defense into the following verified printed page ranges:

Curriculum Subject Korean Term Volume 5 Page Range Pedagogical Focus & Description
Three-Step Sparring 삼보 맞서기 (Sambo Matsogi) pp. 21–90 Fundamental distance, body shifting, basic blocks, and counter-attacks across 3 advancing punches.
Two-Step Sparring 이보 맞서기 (Ibo Matsogi) pp. 91–106 Intermediate combination sparring pairing hand attacks with kicking attacks.
One-Step Sparring 일보 맞서기 (Ilbo Matsogi) pp. 108–223 Decisive self-defense against a single committed attack utilizing vital-point counter-strikes and dodges.

Instructional Diagram Reference Codes (<698>, <699>, <708>)

The private notes contain numeric tags (<698>, <699>, <708>) that indicate instructional manual illustration numbers. The specific originating print publication has not been identified; mappings below represent descriptive contents of those sections in the baseline notes:

  • <698>: Prone-position defense sequences (laying on back against front choke, rear choke, and stomping kick).
  • <699>: Chair defense sequences (seated defenses against lunging punch, elbow choke, rear choke, and rear armbar).
  • <708>: Kneeling-position defense sequences (both practitioners on one knee against spear-finger, punch, and downward knife-hand).

3. Selected Kukkiwon Technical Category Crosswalk

Conceptual Classification Analogue

Kukkiwon's official Taekwondo Technical Terminology Glossary organizes martial-art techniques into ten broad classes. The following selected categories are used in this study guide as functional analogues:

  • Ppaegi (빼기): Freeing grabbed limbs (wrist, collar, arms, body).
  • Kkeokgi (꺾기): Twisting and pressing joint manipulation (wrist locks, armbars, knee locks).
  • Neomgigi (넘기기): Throwing and sweeping techniques (including Ogeum-georeo-neomgigi knee sweeps).
  • Chigi (치기): Striking techniques generally (using palm heels, elbows, knees, and knifehands).
