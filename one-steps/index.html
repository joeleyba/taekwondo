One-Step Sparring & Takedowns Guide

Complete White Belt to 1st Dan Black Belt One-Step Sparring & Counter-Attack Progression

White Belt One-Steps
White Belt
Symbolizes purity and innocence in the beginning student.
  • 1. Rising block, reverse punch
  • 2. Horse riding stance, push block, low, middle, high punch
  • 3. Inner forearm block, back leg front kick
  • 4. Grab wrist, back leg roundhouse kick
Yellow Belt One-Steps
Yellow Belt
Symbolizes the fertile soil from which plants will grow.
  • 1. Outer block, grab wrist, back leg front kick, elbow strike
  • 2. Step forward to inside (horse riding stance), knife hand strike and knife hand block at the same time (Pizza Man)
  • 3. Side kick, knife hand strike to neck
  • 4. Arm up, step forward to inside (horse riding stance), reverse inner forearm block, slapping chest at same time, left hand grab wrist, right hand knife hand strike to neck, reach behind head and pull forward, right knee to stomach
Orange Belt One-Steps
Orange Belt
Represents the sunrise as the student begins to absorb technique.
  • 1. X-block, grab and twist wrist, round kick to the midsection, sweep with same leg, punch as opponent falls
  • 2. Inside out crescent kick, opposite leg low and high round kicks
  • 3. Jump front kick, landing with outer forearm block and punch at the same time
  • 4. Step forward to outside (horse riding stance), push block, punch, inside out crescent kick
Green Belt One-Steps
Green Belt
Symbolizes the green plant that has started and will continue to grow.
  • 1. Outside in crescent kick, opposite leg spin heel kick (both sides)
  • 2. Outside in crescent kick, same leg side kick without touching ground (both sides)
  • 3. Step forward to inside (horse riding stance), left arm outer forearm block and right elbow to temple, both hands grab behind head, head butt, left leg round knee to ribs
  • 4. Step forward to outside (horse riding stance), left arm outer forearm block, left hand reaches behind head, right elbow strike to temple, slide left hand down in front of shoulder and punch with hand, pull with left leg, takedown, right leg round kick on ground
  • 5. Open hand by ear, step out to side, same time circle down punch block, opposite ridge hand to head
Purple Belt One-Steps
Purple Belt
Represents the deepening sky as the student matures in technical execution.
  • 1. Hook kick, same leg round kick, opposite leg spin heel kick
  • 2. Jump up round kick (both sides)
  • 3. Inner block, backfist, elbow
  • 4. Inner block, elbow, step back, chop to neck
Blue Belt One-Steps
Blue Belt
Represents the sky to which the plant shall mature.
  • 1. Inner Block -> Back Fist -> Elbow -> Spin -> Elbow
  • 2. Drop down on left knee, hands on floor, right leg round kick to body, immediately hook behind forward leg and sweep to ground, left hand reverse punch while on knee
  • 3. Step back to front stance, X-block, fold right hand over opponent's wrist in clockwise manner, right leg front kick to face, elbow to spine
  • 4. Brush Block -> Double Round Kick -> Jump Spin Crescent Kick
Red Belt One-Steps
Red Belt
Represents danger, cautions the student to use control, and warns the opponent to stay away.
  • 1. Inner Block -> Back Fist -> Spin Back Fist -> Spin Hook Kick
  • 2. Rising Block -> Groin Attack -> Shift Eye Attack
  • 3. Coffee Grinder -> Spin Sweep
  • 4. Push Block -> Jump Spin Side Kick
  • 5. Brush Block -> Tornado Kick -> Spin Hook Kick
  • 6. Ham Sandwich -> Bust Elbow -> Elbow to Ribs -> Spin Around and Take Down
Brown Belt One-Steps
Brown Belt
Represents stability and solid technical foundation prior to black belt rank.
  • 1. Double side kick, spin double side kick (both sides)
  • 2. Hand open by ear, step back push block (back stance), jump spin heel kick (both sides)
  • 3. Hand open by ear, step back push block (back stance), spin side kick low, same leg round kick high (both sides)
  • 4. Outside-in crescent kick, spin heel kick, round kick (both sides)
  • 5. Drop down on left knee, hands on floor, right leg round kick to body, same leg behind forward leg, jump up, spin left leg to mid-section, takedown, right ridge hand
Brown Belt Black Stripe One-Steps
Brown Belt Black Stripe
The final stage before black belt, demonstrating advanced weapons, breaking, and self-defense.
  • 1. Front kick low, round kick high, opposite leg for each, kick simultaneously (both sides)
  • 2. Inside-out crescent kick, opposite leg outside-in crescent kick, double punch (both sides)
  • 3. Jump up hook kick
  • 4. Hand up in X-block (right hand on top), left foot step out, right leg side kick, armpit, then knee, behind knee for takedown
  • 5. Hands held behind head (knife hands), step back to back stance, knife hand guarding block low, 360 degree side kick
1st Dan Black Belt One-Steps & Takedowns
1st Dan Black Belt
Symbolizes maturity and mastery in the student, unharmed by darkness.
  • Three Steps 1: Punch 1 inner forearm back fist, Punch 2 low push block, Punch 3 circle block ridge hand
  • Three Steps 2: Punch 1 open hand brush block, Punch 2 open hand brush block, Punch 3 jump spin side kick
  • Three Steps 3: Punch 1 rising block punch, Punch 2 rising block kick to groin, Punch 3 grab fist with both hands, twist and break arm over shoulder
  • Three Steps 4: Punch 1 push block punch, Punch 2 push block punch, Punch 3 push block move behind opponent and sleeper hold or snap neck
  • 1st Dan Takedown 1: Step forward rising block, grab wrist bending arm to submission, rear leg sweep
  • 1st Dan Takedown 2: Step forward elbow block and ridge hand, drop rear knee and sweep front leg
