Syllabus Structure & Lineage Architecture

The one-step sparring curriculum represents a composite system pairing early Chang-Hon/General Choi distance-and-timing fundamentals (White to Orange Belts) with Kukkiwon situational self-defense, knee strikes, sweeping takedowns (Neomgigi), joint locks (Kkeokgi), and school-specific tactical combinations (Purple to 1st Dan).

Belt-by-Belt One-Step Sparring Syllabus

White Belt: Fundamental Interceptions & Counters
  • #1: Step back into right walking stance, Left High Rising Block, Right Middle Reverse Punch.
  • #2: Step right into Horse Riding Stance, Left Middle Palm Pushing Block, Right Punch, Left Punch.
  • #3: Step back into right L-stance, Left Inner Forearm Block, Right Front Snap Kick, Right Punch.
Yellow Belt: Angular Evacuation & Kicking Counters
  • #1: Step 45° left into left walking stance, Right Knifehand Block, Right Reverse Punch to ribs.
  • #2: Step 45° right into right walking stance, Left Knifehand Block, Left Roundhouse Kick to ribs.
  • #3: Step outside, Right Middle Side Kick, Right Knifehand Neck Strike.
  • #4: Step inside, deflect punch, Right Knifehand Neck Strike, pull head downward into Right Upward Knee Strike.
Orange Belt: Crescent Interceptions & Spinning Trajectories
  • #1: Inside-to-Outside Crescent Kick to deflect punch, land forward with Right Reverse Punch.
  • #2: Inside-to-Outside Crescent Kick deflection, followed by opposite-leg Roundhouse Kick.
  • #3: Outside-to-Inside Crescent Kick to neck, spin into Back Thrust Kick.
Green Belt: Head Control, Elbows & Sweeps
  • #1: Step inside, double knifehand block, simultaneous throat strike.
  • #2: Deflect, step inside with Right Elbow Strike to ribs, followed by Left Backfist to face.
  • #3: Elbow deflection, seize head, downward headbutt/control into Right Knee Strike.
  • #4: Head control, right elbow strike, leg trip takedown (Neomgigi), follow-up ground kick.
  • #5: Circular downward punch deflection into Right Ridgehand Strike to neck.
Purple & Blue Belt: Multi-Kick Chains & Ground Sweeps
  • Purple #1: Hook Kick into Roundhouse Kick into Spinning Heel Kick (same leg chain).
  • Purple #3: Inner Forearm Block, Right Backfist to bridge of nose, Right Elbow Strike to solar plexus.
  • Blue #2: Drop to ground, low ground Round Kick to ankle, sweeping leg trip, kneeling reverse punch.
  • Blue #3: High X-Block, wrist lock manipulation (Kkeokgi), front kick, right side-elbow strike.
Red Belt & 1st Dan: Tactical Combinations & Three-Step Sparring
  • Red & Brown-Black Series: Multi-angle jumping kicks, spinning heel kick counters, and joint-lock immobilizations.
  • 1st Dan Three-Step Routines: Traditional three-attack advancing sequence ending with sweeping takedowns, standing armbars, and joint locks.
  • Mnemonic Combinations: Specialized tactical sequences ("Coffee Grinder" low circular sweep, "Pizza Man", "Ham Sandwich", and "Bust Elbow").
