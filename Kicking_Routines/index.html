Kicking Routines & Defenses Guide

Complete White Belt to 1st Dan Black Belt Kicking Progression & Counter-Kicks

White Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
White Belt
Symbolizes purity and innocence in the beginning student.
  • Routine Back leg front kick
  • Routine Back leg round kick
  • Routine Front leg side kick
Yellow Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Yellow Belt
Symbolizes the fertile soil from which plants will grow.
  • Routine Front kick, moving forward back leg round kick
  • Routine Jab punch, back leg side kick
  • Routine Moving forward spin side kick
Orange Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Orange Belt
Represents the sunrise as the student begins to absorb technique.
  • Routine Moving forward back leg front kick, double punch
  • Routine Back fist, sliding forward front leg hook kick
  • Routine Back leg round kick, side kick
Green Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Green Belt
Symbolizes the green plant that has started and will continue to grow.
  • Routine Circle block, step forward, back leg front kick
  • Routine Back fist, reverse punch, back leg round kick, step down, spin crescent kick
  • Routine Back leg inside out crescent kick, same leg side kick without setting down, spin hook kick
Purple Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Purple Belt
Represents the deepening sky as the student matures in technical execution.
  • Routine Back leg axe kick, spin side kick
  • Routine Back leg hook kick low, balance and same leg round kick high
  • Routine Spin hook kick, side kick
Blue Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Blue Belt
Represents the sky to which the plant shall mature.
  • Routine Jab punch, reverse punch, back leg round kick, jump spin hook kick
  • Routine Front leg front kick, jump back leg round kick
  • Routine Back leg round kick, hook kick, side kick
  • Routine Crescent, crescent, jump side
Red Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Red Belt
Represents danger, cautions the student to use control, and warns the opponent to stay away.
  • Routine Back fist punch, back leg round kick landing in front, tornado kick
  • Routine Spin hook kick, back leg round kick, jump spin crescent kick
  • Routine Front leg front kick, balancing high round kick, landing on left knee spin around and leg sweep
  • Routine Tornado kick execution
  • Defense Front kick defense: Outer forearm block, front leg front kick to groin
  • Defense Side kick defense: Fall on left knee while balancing with hands, leg sweep opponent's balancing leg
  • Defense Jump spinning kick defense: Slide in close, punch opponent in face
Brown Belt - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Brown Belt
Represents stability and solid technical foundation prior to black belt rank.
  • Routine Front leg front kick, jump spin tornado round kick, back leg side kick landing forward
  • Routine Back leg low, middle, and high side kicks, set down in front
  • Routine Jump spin hook kick
  • Routine Breaking: Jump spin hook kick & Palm heel strike through one brick
  • Defense Axe kick defense: X-block, twist leg and drive opponent to ground
  • Defense Outside-in crescent kick defense: Move in, grab behind opponent's neck and elbow face
  • Defense Hook kick defense: Fall to ground, scissors takedown
Brown Belt Black Stripe - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Brown Belt Black Stripe
The final stage before black belt, demonstrating advanced weapons, breaking, and self-defense.
  • Routine Low knife hand guarding block, 360 degree jump side kick
  • Routine Jump front leg front kick, back leg round kick
  • Routine Jump back leg round kick and land in front
  • Routine Breaking: 360-degree side kick into two boards & Punch through two boards
  • Defense Side kick defense: Augmented block, reach hand behind neck and elbow face
  • Defense Inside-out crescent kick defense: Push block leg while moving to side, side kick
Black Belt & 1st Dan - Kicks & Kicking Defenses
Black Belt & 1st Dan
Symbolizes maturity and mastery in the student, unharmed by darkness.
  • Routine Low knife hand guarding block, 360 degree hook kick
  • Routine Jump split front kick
  • Routine Jump back leg round kick, 360 degree side kick
  • Routine Breaking: 360-degree spin hook kick & Hammer fist through one brick
  • Routine 1st Dan Breaking: Split front kick through two boards, Jump sidekick 360° kick, Jump scissors kick, Elbow through one brick
