HWA-RANG (화랑)

29 Movements | Ready Stance: Closed Ready Stance C (Moa Junbi Sogi C)
Brown Belt Form #8

Pattern Meaning: Named after the Hwa-Rang youth group originating in the Silla Dynasty in the early 7th century. The 29 movements refer to the 29th Infantry Division where Taekwon-Do matured.

Return Foot: Right Foot returns to Ready Stance.

Key Form Structure & Memory Anchors:
  • Starts in Close Ready Stance C (Moa Junbi Sogi C).
  • Moves 1-3 (facing D in Sitting Stance): Palm Pushing Block (L) -> Middle Punch (R) -> Middle Punch (L).
  • Move 6: Middle Punch to A in Right Fixed Stance with sliding motion.
  • Move 7: Left Vertical Stance Downward Strike with Right Knife-Hand to A.
  • Move 11: Moa Sogi posture with Left Palm to Right Forefist (Elbow 45° outward).
  • Move 12: Middle Side Piercing Kick (R) to D, land in Left L-Stance with Right Knife-Hand Middle Side Strike to D.
  • Moves 18-19: Fast Motion High Turning Kicks to DF (R leg) & CF (L leg).
  • Move 25: Right Side Elbow Thrust to C in Right L-Stance (sliding motion).
