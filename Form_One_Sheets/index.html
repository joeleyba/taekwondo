Forms & Poomsae Suite
Form #1 White Belt
CHON-JI (천지)
Chon-Ji literally means 'Heaven and Earth'. In the Orient, it is interpreted as the creation of the world or the beginning of human history, performed by the beginner. Consists of two similar parts (Heaven & Earth).
19 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #2 Yellow Belt
DAN-GUN (단군)
Named after the Holy Dan-Gun, the legendary founder of Korea in the year 2333 BC.
21 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #3 Orange Belt
DO-SAN (도산)
Do-San is the pseudonym of patriot An Chang-Ho (1876-1938), who devoted his life to furthering education in Korea and its independence movement.
24 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #4 Green Belt
WON-HYO (원효)
Won-Hyo was the noted monk who introduced Buddhism to the Silla Dynasty in the year 686 AD.
28 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #5 Purple Belt
YUL-GOK (율곡)
Yul-Gok is the pseudonym of the great philosopher and scholar Yi I (1536-1584), nicknamed the 'Confucius of Korea'. The 38 movements refer to his birthplace on 38° latitude.
38 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #6 Blue Belt
JOONG-GUN (중근)
Named after patriot An Joong-Gun who assassinated Hiro-Bumi Ito, the Japanese governor-general. The 32 movements represent Mr. An's age when executed in 1910.
32 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #7 Red Belt
TOI-GYE (퇴계)
Toi-Gye is the pen name of scholar Yi Hwang (16th century), authority on Neo-Confucianism. The 37 movements refer to his birthplace on 37° latitude.
37 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #8 Brown Belt
HWA-RANG (화랑)
Named after the Hwa-Rang youth group originating in the Silla Dynasty in the early 7th century. The 29 movements refer to the 29th Infantry Division where Taekwon-Do matured.
29 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #9 Red/Black Belt
CHOONG-MOO (충무)
Named after Admiral Yi Sun-Sin of the Yi Dynasty, reputed inventor of the armored battleship (Kobukson) in 1592. Ends with a left-hand attack to symbolize his regrettable death.
30 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #10 1st Dan Black Belt
KWANG-GAE (광개)
Named after Kwang-Gae-Toh-Wang, the 19th King of the Koguryo Dynasty who expanded Korean territory. The 39 movements refer to 391 AD when he came to throne.
39 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #11 1st Dan Black Belt (Bridge Form)
KORYO (고려)
Named after the Koryo Dynasty (918-1392 AD) from which the name 'Korea' was derived. Koryo symbolizes 'Seonbae' (a learned man of virtue and strong martial spirit). Serves as a vital bridge between ITF and Kukkiwon / World Taekwondo systems.
30 Movements Return: Left Foot
