YUL-GOK (율곡)

38 Movements | Ready Stance: Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Purple Belt Form #5

Pattern Meaning: Yul-Gok is the pseudonym of the great philosopher and scholar Yi I (1536-1584), nicknamed the 'Confucius of Korea'. The 38 movements refer to his birthplace on 38° latitude.

Return Foot: Left Foot returns to Ready Stance.

D=Front | C=Back | A=Right | B=Left
Key Form Structure & Memory Anchors:
  • Moves 1-6: Sitting Stance Middle Punch (Extending & Fast Motion).
  • Moves 15-17 & 18-20: Palm Hooking Block -> Reverse Hooking Block -> Middle Punch.
  • Moves 22-24 & 25-27: Bending Stance A -> Side Piercing Kick -> Front Elbow Strike.

Part 1 (Moves 1 - 6) - Sitting Stance Punches & Fast Motion
1 D Move L foot to B Sitting Stance Extend Left Fist Horizontally Middle
2 D Maintain Stance Sitting Stance Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
3 D Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Sitting Stance Middle Reverse Punch (L) Middle
4 D Bring L foot to R, Move R foot to A Sitting Stance Extend Right Fist Horizontally Middle
5 D Maintain Stance Sitting Stance Middle Obverse Punch (L) Middle
6 D Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Sitting Stance Middle Reverse Punch (R) Middle
Part 2 (Moves 7 - 14) - Inner Forearm Side Blocks, Front Kicks & Punches
7 AD Move R foot to AD Right Walking High Inner Forearm Side Block (R) High
8 AD Kick to AD Front Snap Kick Low Front Snap Kick (L) Low
9 AD Lower L foot to AD Left Walking Middle Obverse Punch (L) Middle
10 AD Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Left Walking Middle Reverse Punch (R) Middle
11 BD Move L foot to BD Left Walking High Inner Forearm Side Block (L) High
12 BD Kick to BD Front Snap Kick Low Front Snap Kick (R) Low
13 BD Lower R foot to BD Right Walking Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
14 BD Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Right Walking Middle Reverse Punch (L) Middle
Part 3 (Moves 15 - 21) - Palm Hooking Blocks & Marching Punches
15 D Move R foot to D (Pivot L foot) Right Walking High Palm Hooking Block (R) High
16 D Maintain Stance Right Walking High Palm Hooking Block (L) High
17 D Maintain Stance Right Walking Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
18 D Move L foot to D Left Walking High Palm Hooking Block (L) High
19 D Maintain Stance Left Walking High Palm Hooking Block (R) High
20 D Continuous Motion (Maintain stance) Left Walking Middle Obverse Punch (L) Middle
21 D Move R foot to D Right Walking Middle Obverse Punch (R) KIHAP Middle
Part 4 (Moves 22 - 27) - Bending Stances, Side Kicks & Elbow Strikes
22 D Turn Face to D (Pivot R leg) Right Bending Ready Stance A Bending Balance Guard Middle
23 D Kick to D Side Kick Middle Side Piercing Kick (L) Middle
24 D Lower L foot to D Left Walking Right Front Elbow Strike into Left Palm Middle
25 C Turn Face to C (Pivot L leg) Left Bending Ready Stance A Bending Balance Guard Middle
26 C Kick to C Side Kick Middle Side Piercing Kick (R) Middle
27 C Lower R foot to C Right Walking Left Front Elbow Strike into Right Palm Middle
Part 5 (Moves 28 - 38) - Twin Knifehand, Fingertip Thrusts & Jump X-Stance
28 E Move L foot to E Right L-Stance Twin Knife-Hand Block Middle
29 E Move R foot to E Right Walking Middle Straight Fingertip Thrust (R) Middle
30 F Move R foot to F (Turn Clockwise) Left L-Stance Twin Knife-Hand Block Middle
31 F Move L foot to F Left Walking Middle Straight Fingertip Thrust (L) Middle
32 C Move L foot to C Left Walking High Outer Forearm Side Block (L) High
33 C Maintain Stance Left Walking Middle Reverse Punch (R) Middle
34 C Move R foot to C Right Walking High Outer Forearm Side Block (R) High
35 C Maintain Stance Right Walking Middle Reverse Punch (L) Middle
36 C Jump to C Left X-Stance (facing B) High Backfist Side Strike (L) KIHAP High
37 A Move R foot to A Right Walking High Double Forearm Block (R) High
38 B Bring R foot to L, Move L foot to B Left Walking High Double Forearm Block (L) High
