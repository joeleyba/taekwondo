KORYO (고려)

30 Movements | Ready Stance: Tongmilgi Junbi-Seogi (Barrel Pushing Ready Stance)
1st Dan Black Belt (Kukkiwon Poomsae) Form #11

Pattern Meaning: Named after the Koryo Dynasty (918-1392 AD). Symbolizes 'Seonbae' (learned man of virtue and martial spirit). Kukkiwon 1st Dan Poomsae.

Return Foot: Left Foot returns to Ready Stance.

Key Form Structure & Memory Anchors:
  • 30 individually counted Kukkiwon poomsae movements.
  • Kukkiwon Stances: Dwitkubi (L-Stance equivalent), Apkubi (Front Stance), Juchum-Seogi (Sitting Stance), Ap-Seogi (Short Stance).
  • Moves 2-3 & 8-9: Kodeup Yop-Chagi (Low Side Kick followed by High Side Kick).
  • Moves 15 & 30: Agwison / Khaljaebi (Arc-Hand Throat Thrust).

Part 1 (Moves 1 - 6) - Double Knifehand, Double Side Kick & Inner Block to B
1 B Step L foot to B Right Dwitkubi Sonnal Momtong-Makki (Double Knife-Hand Middle Guarding Block) Middle
2 B Kick to B (Lead Leg) Side Kick Low Yop-Chagi (Low Side Kick) Low
3 B Kick to B without touching down Side Kick High Yop-Chagi (High Side Kick) High
4 B Lower R foot to B Right Apkubi Sonnal Bakkat-Chigi (Right Knife-Hand High Outside Strike) High
5 B Maintain Stance Right Apkubi Momtong Jireugi (Right Middle Punch) Middle
6 B Pull R foot in slightly Left Dwitkubi Anpalmok Momtong-Makki (Left Inner Forearm Middle Block) Middle
Part 2 (Moves 7 - 12) - Double Knifehand, Double Side Kick & Inner Block to A
7 A Turn 180° Clockwise (Step R foot to A) Left Dwitkubi Sonnal Momtong-Makki (Double Knife-Hand Middle Guarding Block) Middle
8 A Kick to A (Lead Leg) Side Kick Low Yop-Chagi (Low Side Kick) Low
9 A Kick to A without touching down Side Kick High Yop-Chagi (High Side Kick) High
10 A Lower L foot to A Left Apkubi Sonnal Bakkat-Chigi (Left Knife-Hand High Outside Strike) High
11 A Maintain Stance Left Apkubi Momtong Jireugi (Left Middle Punch) Middle
12 A Pull L foot in slightly Right Dwitkubi Anpalmok Momtong-Makki (Right Inner Forearm Middle Block) Middle
Part 3 (Moves 13 - 17) - Low Block, Arc-Hand Thrusts & Knee Joint Breaking
13 D Turn 90° Left to D (Step L foot) Left Apkubi Sonnal Arae-Makki (Left Single Knife-Hand Low Block) Low
14 D Maintain Stance Left Apkubi Khaljaebi / Agwison (Right Arc-Hand / Tiger Mouth Thrust to Throat) High
15 D Front Snap Kick to D & Land Forward Right Apkubi Front Snap Kick (R) & Sonnal Arae-Makki + Khaljaebi (L) High
16 D Front Snap Kick to D & Land Forward Left Apkubi Front Snap Kick (L) & Sonnal Arae-Makki + Khaljaebi (R) KIHAP High
17 D Front Snap Kick to D & Land Forward Right Apkubi Front Snap Kick (R) & Mureup-Kkeokki (Right Knee Joint Breaking Press) Low
Part 4 (Moves 18 - 24) - Wedging Push Block, Sitting Stance Target Punch & Side Kick
18 C Turn 180° Right to C (Step L foot) Left Apkubi Anpalmok Hecho-Makki (Inner Forearm Wedging Push Block) Middle
19 C Front Snap Kick to C & Land Forward Left Apkubi Front Snap Kick (L) & Mureup-Kkeokki (Left Knee Joint Breaking) Low
20 C Pull L foot in slightly Left Ap-Seogi Anpalmok Hecho-Makki (Inner Forearm Wedging Push Block) Middle
21 B Turn 90° Right to B (Step down in Sitting Stance) Juchum-Seogi (facing D) Left Single Knife-Hand Middle Side Block to B Middle
22 B Maintain Stance Juchum-Seogi (facing D) Pyojeok-Jireugi (Right Target Punch into Left Open Palm to B) Middle
23 B Cross R foot over L foot & Side Kick to B Apkkoa-Seogi Yop-Chagi (L Side Kick) & Pyonsonkkeut Arae Jecho-Tzireugi (L Spear Hand) Low
24 A Pull R foot in slightly Right Ap-Seogi (facing A) Arae-Makki (Right Low Outer Forearm Block) Low
Part 5 (Moves 25 - 30) - Elbow Side Strike, Target Hammer Fist & Final Throat Thrust
25 A Step L foot 1 step forward to A Juchum-Seogi (facing D) Right Palm Heel Downward Block & Left Palkup Pyojeok-Chigi (Side Elbow) Middle
26 A Maintain Stance Juchum-Seogi (facing D) Right Single Knife-Hand Middle Side Block to A Middle
27 A Maintain Stance Juchum-Seogi (facing D) Left Pyojeok-Jireugi (Target Punch into Right Open Palm to A) Middle
28 A Cross L foot over R foot & Side Kick to A Apkkoa-Seogi Yop-Chagi (R Side Kick) & Pyonsonkkeut Arae Jecho-Tzireugi (R Spear Hand) Low
29 D Bring R foot to L foot Moa-Seogi (facing D) Mejumeok Arae Pyojeok-Chigi (Left Hammer-Fist Low Target Strike) Low
30 C Turn 90° Left to C (Step R foot forward) Right Apkubi Khaljaebi / Agwison (Right Arc-Hand / Tiger Mouth Thrust to Throat) KIHAP High
