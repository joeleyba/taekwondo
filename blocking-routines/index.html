Belt-by-Belt Hand & Blocking Combinations

White Belt

  1. Down block (Low outer forearm block) [하단막기 / Hadan Makgi / Najunde Bakkat Palmok Makki]
  2. Rising block (High outer forearm rising block) [상단막기 / Sangdan Makgi / Chookyo Makki]
  3. Outer forearm block [바깥팔목막기 / Bakkat Palmok Makki]
  4. Inner forearm block [안팔목막기 / An Palmok Makki]

Yellow Belt

  1. Down block, double punch [Arae Makgi, Doo-beon Jireugi]
  2. Rising block, double punch [Eolgool Makgi, Doo-beon Jireugi]
  3. Knife hand guarding block [손날수도막기 / Sonnal Daebi Makki]
  4. Twin forearm block [쌍팔목막기 / Sang Palmok Makki]
  5. Knife hand strike [손날치기 / Sonnal Chigi]

Orange Belt

  1. Down block, rising block, double punch [Arae Makgi, Eolgool Makgi, Doo-beon Jireugi]
  2. Rising block, down block, double punch [Eolgool Makgi, Arae Makgi, Doo-beon Jireugi]
  3. Knife hand guarding block, double punch [Sonnal Daebi Makki, Doo-beon Jireugi]
  4. Twin forearm block, point, punch [Sang Palmok Makki, Pyojeok Jireugi]
  5. Knife hand strike, punch [Sonnal Chigi, Jireugi]

Green Belt

  1. Twin forearm block, sliding to cat stance, reverse knife hand strike, point, punch [Sang Palmok Makki, Beom-seogi Sonnal-deung Chigi]
  2. Moving forward circle block, reverse punch [Dollimyo Makki, Bandae Jireugi]
  3. Knife hand guarding block, down block, reverse punch [Sonnal Daebi Makki, Arae Makgi, Bandae Jireugi]
  4. Circle block, same hand knife hand strike, step forward reverse knife hand strike to neck [Dollimyo Makki, Sonnal Mok-chigi, Sonnal-deung Mok-chigi]

Purple Belt

  1. Outer forearm block, knife hand guarding block, reverse punch [Bakkat Palmok Makki, Sonnal Daebi Makki, Bandae Jireugi]
  2. Search block, opposite hand search block, then punch [Tamseak Makki, Jireugi]
  3. Outer forearm block, front stance punch [Bakkat Palmok Makki, Apkubi Jireugi]
  4. Twin knife hand block, step forward spear finger [Sang Sonnal Makki, Sun Sonkut Tulgi]

Blue Belt (From Form)

  1. Reverse knife hand block, front leg front kick, stepping forward rising palm heel strike in a cat stance [Sonnal-deung Makki, Ap-chagi, Batangson Ollyo-chigi]
  2. Knife hand guarding block, shifting into a front stance, right elbow [Sonnal Daebi Makki, Apkubi Palkup Chigi]
  3. Twin high punch, stepping forward, twin upset punch [Sang Sangdan Jireugi, Sang Dwijibo Jireugi]
  4. Augmented block in a front stance, shift to horse riding stance, pull back punch, move forward and side kick [Geodeureo Makki, Juchum-seogi Jireugi, Yeop-chagi]
  5. Move to a fighting stance, shift to a front stance and execute pressing palm blocks [Batangson Noollo Makki]

Red Belt

  1. Back stance outer forearm block, shifting to front stance open hand groin strike [Dwitkubi Bakkat Makki, Apkubi Sonbadak Simjang Chigi]
  2. Step, low X-block in a front stance, pull back twin high punch [Apkubi Kyocha Makki, Sang Sangdan Jireugi]
  3. Step back, high right back fist and low left down block in a back stance [Dwitkubi Deung-jumeok & Arae Makgi]
  4. Knife hand guarding block in a back stance, pointing right knife hand, balance front leg front kick, pull back right hand and left hand spear finger [Sonnal Daebi Makki, Pyojeok Ap-chagi, Sun Sonkut Tulgi]
  5. Low knife hand sudo block (both sides) [Han-sonnal Arae Makgi]

Brown Belt

  1. Back stance twin forearm block, pull left fist to ear and inner forearm block while pulling right fist to chest [Dwitkubi Sang Palmok Makki, An Palmok Makki]
  2. Back stance inner forearm block, switch to front stance and inner forearm block with opposite hand [Dwitkubi An Palmok Makki, Apkubi An Palmok Makki]
  3. Lead hand jab, reverse punch, jump spin back fist [Bap-jumeok, Bandae Jireugi, Twimyo Dollyeo Deung-jumeok]
  4. Twin inverted knife hand strike to neck, twin low knife hand block in front stance, step forward into back stance and knife hand strike [Sang Sonnal Mok-chigi, Sang Sonnal Arae Makgi, Sonnal Chigi]

Brown Belt Black Stripe

  1. Twin knife hand guarding block, step forward inverted chop at neck level while other hand is over forehead [Sonnal Daebi Makki, Sonnal Mok-chigi]
  2. Back stance low block, fold hands by ear, grab neck and knee [Dwitkubi Arae Makgi, Mureup Chigi]
  3. Twin stick block, jump spin knife hand guarding block [Ssang Mongdung-i Makki, Twimyo Sonnal Daebi Makki]
  4. Augmented block in front stance, pivot on left foot, move to horse riding stance and right inside block, back fist with right hand [Geodeureo Makki, Juchum-seogi An Makki & Deung-jumeok]
  5. Stepping forward double palm strike [Sang Batangson Chigi]

Black Belt Stage

  1. Low block (open hand), throat strike, back leg front kick [Han-sonnal Arae Makgi, Kaljaebi, Dwi-bal Ap-chagi]
  2. Knife hand guarding block, shift to front stance and punch, shift to back stance and inner forearm block [Sonnal Daebi Makki, Apkubi Jireugi, Dwitkubi An Palmok Makki]
  3. Low groin strike, twin forearm block in front stance [Simjang Chigi, Apkubi Sang Palmok Makki]
  4. Knife hand guarding block (back stance), hook punch into hand, step into side kick [Sonnal Daebi Makki, Dollyeo-jireugi, Yeop-chagi]
  5. Low block (open hand), inverted knife hand strike [Han-sonnal Arae Makgi, Sonnal-deung Chigi]
