Belt-by-Belt Hand & Blocking Combinations

White Belt

  1. Down block (Low outer forearm block)
  2. Rising block (High outer forearm rising block)
  3. Outer forearm block
  4. Inner forearm block

Yellow Belt

  1. Down block, double punch
  2. Rising block, double punch
  3. Knife hand guarding block
  4. Twin forearm block
  5. Knife hand strike

Orange Belt

  1. Down block, rising block, double punch
  2. Rising block, down block, double punch
  3. Knife hand guarding block, double punch
  4. Twin forearm block, point, punch
  5. Knife hand strike, punch

Green Belt

  1. Twin forearm block, sliding to cat stance, reverse knife hand strike, point, punch
  2. Moving forward circle block, reverse punch
  3. Knife hand guarding block, down block, reverse punch
  4. Circle block, same hand knife hand strike, step forward reverse knife hand strike to neck

Purple Belt

  1. Outer forearm block, knife hand guarding block, reverse punch
  2. Search block, opposite hand search block, then punch
  3. Outer forearm block, front stance punch
  4. Twin knife hand block, step forward spear finger

Blue Belt (From Form)

  1. Reverse knife hand block, front leg front kick, stepping forward rising palm heel strike in a cat stance
  2. Knife hand guarding block, shifting into a front stance, right elbow
  3. Twin high punch, stepping forward, twin upset punch
  4. Augmented block in a front stance, shift to horse riding stance, pull back punch, move forward and side kick
  5. Move to a fighting stance, shift to a front stance and execute pressing palm blocks

Red Belt

  1. Back stance outer forearm block, shifting to front stance open hand groin strike
  2. Step, low X-block in a front stance, pull back twin high punch
  3. Step back, high right back fist and low left down block in a back stance
  4. Knife hand guarding block in a back stance, pointing right knife hand, balance front leg front kick, pull back right hand and left hand spear finger
  5. Low knife hand sudo block (both sides)

Brown Belt

  1. Back stance twin forearm block, pull left fist to ear and inner forearm block while pulling right fist to chest
  2. Back stance inner forearm block, switch to front stance and inner forearm block with opposite hand
  3. Lead hand jab, reverse punch, jump spin back fist
  4. Twin inverted knife hand strike to neck, twin low knife hand block in front stance, step forward into back stance and knife hand strike

Brown Belt Black Stripe

  1. Twin knife hand guarding block, step forward inverted chop at neck level while other hand is over forehead
  2. Back stance low block, fold hands by ear, grab neck and knee
  3. Twin stick block, jump spin knife hand guarding block
  4. Augmented block in front stance, pivot on left foot, move to horse riding stance and right inside block, back fist with right hand
  5. Stepping forward double palm strike

Black Belt Stage

  1. Low block (open hand), throat strike, back leg front kick
  2. Knife hand guarding block, shift to front stance and punch, shift to back stance and inner forearm block
  3. Low groin strike, twin forearm block in front stance
  4. Knife hand guarding block (back stance), hook punch into hand, step into side kick
  5. Low block (open hand), inverted knife hand strike
