1. White & Yellow Belt: Fundamental Wrist Escapes

White Belt #1

Same-Side Wrist Grab (Direct Pull & Leverage)

Rotate grabbed wrist inward toward opponent's thumb (the weakest point). Step back into walking stance, pull elbow to ribs, and counter with reverse punch.

White Belt #2

Cross-Wrist Grab (Circle & Palm Heel)

Circle grabbed hand over opponent's wrist in a clockwise arc. Push down while delivering a palm heel strike to the chin.

Yellow Belt #1

Two-on-One Wrist Grab (Assisted Release)

Reach across with free hand to clasp own fist. Step back and pull both hands straight up through the thumb gap toward your chest, following with front kick.

2. Green, Purple & Blue Belt: Lapel, Collar & Bearhug Releases

Green Belt

Single Lapel Grab (Wrist Lock Counter)

Cover grabbing hand with two hands, securing opponent's knuckles. Step back, bend wrists down 90° into center chest, forcing opponent to their knees.

Purple Belt

Rear Bearhug (Arms Pinned)

Drop weight into Horse Riding Stance. Deliver double heel stamp to opponent's instep, headbutt rearward to the nose, elbow strike to ribs, turn and execute groin strike.

Blue Belt

Front Two-Hand Choke Release

Raise right arm high, turn shoulders 90° to break the grip leverage. Bring right forearm down hard across both wrists, then counter with elbow strike.

3. Red Belt & 1st Dan: Hapkido Joint Locks, Chair Defenses & Pressure Points

Red Belt

Side Headlock Escape & Throw

Reach over opponent's shoulder with left hand to push nose/chin backward. Strike groin with right hand, hook behind opponent's knee, and sweep them to the floor.

1st Dan Black Belt

Seated Chair Defense against Front Punch

From seated position, deflect punch outward with knifehand block. Stand up driving palm heel under chin while kicking opponent's shin, sweeping into armbar control.
