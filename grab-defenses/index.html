Grab Defenses & Hapkido Self-Defense Guide

Complete Belt-by-Belt Wrist Escapes, Chokes, Bear Hugs & Joint Lock Defenses

White Belt Grab Defenses
White Belt
Basic wrist release leverage points and immediate counter-attacks.
  • 1. Wrist grab (right and left): Pull away toward thumb opening and back fist
  • 2. Opponent grabs both wrists low: Clap upwards, bring hands inside
  • 3. Opponent grabs both wrists high: Clap upwards, twin knifehand strike to neck
  • 4. Straight across wrist grab: Turn wrist and push block with both hands, kick groin
Yellow Belt Grab Defenses
Yellow Belt
Advanced wrist releases, thumb peeling, and standing joint manipulation.
  • 1. Cross wrist grab: Peel thumb, press wrist toward opponent
  • 2. Cross wrist grab: Grab meaty portion of hand, twist and grab elbow with pressure
  • 3. Diagonal wrist grab: Turn both wrists and push block with both hands
  • 4. Opponent grabs wrist with both hands: Take free hand and reach through arms grabbing your fist and step back as you pull out (Hammer fist)
  • 5. Diagonal wrist grab: Grab thumb, pull hand off and use other hand to also grab, circle foot around and take down opponent
  • 6. Attacker lunges with strike or grab: Knife hand block and take down opponent with a behind the leg sweep
Orange Belt Grab Defenses
Orange Belt
Front and rear choke releases and handshake counter-locks.
  • 1. Front choke hold: Grab left wrist, right arm up, step back with left leg, strike with right elbow
  • 2. Rear choke hold: Step behind right foot with left while circling left arm around opponent's arms, punch stomach, elbow face
  • 3. Opponent does a front choke: Step back and block elbow with your inner forearm
  • 4. Opponent chokes from behind: Step sideways as you do an outer forearm block
  • 5. Hand shake: Open up your hand and use your free hand to grab your fingers and step back
  • 6. Hand shake: Use free hand to grab opponent's thumb and circle with opposite foot and pull attacker down
Green Belt Grab Defenses
Green Belt
Bear hug releases (over arms, under arms, high, low).
  • 1. Attacker performs front bear hug over your arms: Take both hands and step and push on pelvic bones
  • 2. Attacker performs front bear hug under your arms: Make phoenix knuckle and push into their ribs
  • 3. Attacker performs a high bear hug: Drop to a horse riding stance, pray and lift up your hands (Hallelujah!)
  • 4. Attacker performs a low bear hug: Strike hand, take heel and dig in on top of their foot
Purple & Blue Belt Grab Defenses
Blue Belt
Headlocks, armbars, and full nelson escapes.
  • 1. Full Nelson: Peel hand off neck, twist, turn and palm strike face
  • 2. Arm bar hold: Step back into attacker -> Elbow 1, 2, 3
  • 3. Attacker grabs both wrists from behind: Step and raise arm up and cross it over the other and escape
  • 4. Attacker places you in headlock: Push on outside of knee with front fist while pushing kidneys with other hand
  • 5. Reverse armbar on neck: Turn into opponent, step forward and sweep opponent while clotheslining
Red & Brown Belt Defenses & Weapons
Red / Brown Belt
Hair grabs, shirt choke releases, and weapon disarms.
  • 1. Front hair grab: Both hands -> Elbow to elbow -> Push down on elbow joint
  • 2. Behind hair pull: Both hands -> Twist -> Elbow to elbow -> Push up to extend elbow
  • 3. Front V-Neck Choke Grab: Weave through with arm -> Press hands together -> Push to side
  • 4. Choke from behind: Raise left arm, spin pivot into back stance, harness arms, left leg sweep
  • 5. Knife attack to chest: Step away from lead leg, grab wrist and twist arm behind back
  • 6. Knife attack to head: X-block, grab wrist, twist and drive knife into attacker
  • 7. Knife attack to midsection: Grab wrist, reach under armpit, grab shoulder and flip
1st Dan Black Belt Hapkido Joint Locks & Positional Defenses
1st Dan Black Belt
Advanced leg bars, shoulder throws, prone, kneeling, and chair self-defense.
  • 1. Leg Bar Hapkido Defense: From right knee, palm heel strike right inside knee while sweeping ankle. Wrap right arm around lower calf and apply small circle wrist extension into Achilles tendon
  • 2. Shoulder Throw: Opponent two hand front choke. Grab right arm with left hand. Swing right arm over left elbow, place inside of right elbow under armpit, squat low and throw over shoulder
  • 3. Arm Lock against across punch: Move outside, hook right wrist around elbow joint, keep forearm trapped to chest and bow forward
  • 4. Prone Position (On Back): Front choke wrist break & elbow press; Rear choke wrist twist; Stomping kick twin palm block and twist
  • 5. Kneeling Position: Spear finger outer forearm block & ankle sweep; Downward knife hand X-fist grab and sweep
  • 6. Chair Defenses: Lunge punch rising block into knee attack; Rear choke arm twist; Rear choke flip; Armbar hold thumb peel and elbow break
  • 7. Nine Fatal Pressure Points: Pai poi, Chi men, Tang men, Navel door, Hui yin, Nao hu, Tien tu, Po lang, Ming men
