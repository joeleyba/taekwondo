1. UMA Curriculum Crosswalk to Selected Kukkiwon Categories

Ppaegi (빼기) - Releases & Escapes

Rotating, slipping, or levering free from wrist, lapel, neck, or body holds by exploiting the attacker's weakest anatomical point (the thumb opening or leveraged angles).

Kkeokgi (꺾기) - Joint Manipulations

Controlling or disabling the attacker by twisting, pressing, bending, or hyper-extending the wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee joints.

Neomgigi (넘기기) - Trips & Takedowns

Unbalancing the opponent through foot sweeps, hip leverage, joint rotation, or leg hooks (such as Ogeum-georeo-neomgigi) to bring them to the ground.

Chigi (치기) - Striking Techniques

Striking techniques generally (using palm heels, elbows, knees, knifehands, or backfists). Kukkiwon separately classifies punching (Jireugi), thrusting (Jjireugi), and kicking (Chagi).

2. Belt-by-Belt Grab Defenses & Releases (Hosin-sul)

White Belt

Fundamental Wrist Releases

Wrist Grab (Right / Left): Pull away against the thumb opening and execute an immediate backfist strike.

Both Wrists Low: Clap hands together upward forcefully to break grip.

Both Wrists High: Clap hands together upward forcefully, followed by twin knife-hand strike to the neck.

Straight Across Wrist Grab: Turn wrist and execute two-hand push block, followed by front kick to groin.

Yellow Belt

Cross-Wrist & Two-on-One Escapes

Cross Wrist Grab (Option 1): Peel thumb and press wrist downward toward opponent.

Cross Wrist Grab (Option 2): Grab meaty portion of hand, twist, and grab opponent's elbow with downward pressure.

Diagonal Wrist Grab: Turn both wrists and execute two-hand push block.

Two Hands on One Wrist: Reach free hand through arms, clasp own fist, step back, and pull straight out (follow with hammerfist).

Diagonal Wrist Grab Takedown: Grab opponent's thumb, pull hand off, circle foot around, and sweep opponent to the ground.

Lunging Strike / Grab Defense: Knife-hand block and take down opponent with behind-the-leg sweep.

Orange Belt

Choke Releases & Handshake Counters

Front Choke Hold: Grab left wrist, raise right arm vertically, step back with left leg, and strike downward with right elbow.

Rear Choke Hold: Step behind right foot with left leg while circling left arm around opponent's arms; punch stomach and elbow face.

Front Choke (Forearm Block): Step back and deflect attacker's elbow with inner forearm block.

Rear Choke (Outer Block): Step sideways and execute outer forearm block to break hold.

Handshake Defense (Fingers): Open hand, use free hand to grab own fingers, and step back to disengage.

Handshake Defense (Takedown): Grab opponent's thumb with free hand, circle opposite foot, and pull attacker to ground.

Green Belt

Bearhug Defenses

Front Bearhug (Over Arms): Step back and push forcefully on opponent's pelvic bones with both hands.

Front Bearhug (Under Arms): Form phoenix-eye knuckles and drive into opponent's floating ribs.

High Bearhug: Drop rapidly into Horse Riding Stance, bring hands together in prayer posture, and lift upward overhead.

Low Bearhug: Strike grabbing hands, drive heel down onto top of opponent's foot (instep stamp).

Purple Belt

Full Nelson, Armbars & Rear Grabs

Full Nelson Defense: Peel grabbing hand off neck, twist, turn into attacker, and deliver palm strike to face.

Armbar Hold Defense: Step back into attacker, delivering three consecutive elbow strikes (Elbow 1, 2, 3).

Both Wrists Grabbed from Behind: Step, raise arm up and cross over the other to disengage leverage.

Blue Belt

Headlock & Neck Defenses

Side Headlock Defense: Push outward on knee joint with lead fist while driving rear hand into kidney area.

Reverse Neck Armbar: Turn into opponent, step forward, sweep opponent's leg, and execute clothesline takedown.

Red Belt

Hair Pulls & Collar Chokes

Front Hair Grab: Place both hands on attacker's hands, align elbow-to-elbow, and press down on elbow joint.

Rear Hair Pull: Place both hands on attacker's grip, twist body, align elbow-to-elbow, and push upward to hyper-extend elbow.

Front V-Neck Choke: Weave arm through grip, press hands together, and drive horizontally to side.

Brown Belt

Rear Harness Sweeps & Ridgehand Counters

Rear Choke Harness Sweep: Raise left arm, spin pivot into back stance harnessing both attacker's arms, grab shoulder, and sweep with left leg.

Ridgehand Strike Defense: Push block with left hand, step forward with left leg sweep, and execute takedown.

3. Weapons Defenses (Brown-Black Stripe & 2nd Dan)

  • Knife Attack to Chest (Brown-Black): Evade away from lead leg, grab attacker's wrist, and twist arm into rear hammerlock.
  • Knife Attack to Head (Brown-Black): High X-block, grab wrist, twist, and redirect knife trajectory toward attacker.
  • Knife Attack to Midsection (Brown-Black): Grab wrist, reach under armpit to grab shoulder, and execute hip leverage flip.
  • Overhead Knife Strike (2nd Dan): Rising block, reach free hand behind wrist, and pull back to force disarm.
  • Reverse Knife to Throat (2nd Dan): Two-hand trap on forearm/wrist, duck under arm, pivot, and disarm/slash.
  • Inverted Knife Attack (2nd Dan): Evade in Horse Riding Stance with push block, execute hand sandwich lock, and twist wrist for disarm.
