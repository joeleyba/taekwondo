1. Kukkiwon Self-Defense Classification Taxonomy

Ppaegi (빼기) - Releases & Escapes

Rotating, slipping, or levering free from wrist, lapel, neck, or body holds by exploiting the attacker's weakest anatomical point (the thumb opening or leveraged angles).

Kkeokgi (꺾기) - Joint Manipulations & Locks

Controlling or disabling the attacker by twisting, pressing, bending, or hyper-extending the wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee joints.

Neomgigi (넘기기) - Trips, Sweeps & Takedowns

Unbalancing the opponent through foot sweeps, hip leverage, joint rotation, or leg hooks (such as Ogeum-georeo-neomgigi) to bring them to the ground.

Chigi (치기) - Counter-Striking

Immediate devastating follow-up strikes utilizing palm heels (Batangson), elbows (Palkup), knees (Mureup), ridgehands, or open-hand strikes to vital areas.

2. White & Yellow Belt: Fundamental Releases (Ppaegi)

White Belt #1 Ppaegi + Chigi

Same-Side Wrist Grab (Direct Pull & Leverage)

Rotate grabbed wrist inward toward opponent's thumb (the weakest point). Step back into walking stance, pull elbow to ribs, and counter with immediate reverse punch.

White Belt #2 Turning Ppaegi + Batangson-Chigi

Cross-Wrist Grab (Circle & Palm Heel)

Circle grabbed hand over opponent's wrist in a clockwise arc. Push downward while stepping forward to deliver a driving palm heel strike (Batangson-Chigi) under the chin.

Yellow Belt #1 Ollyeo-Ppaegi + Ap-Chagi

Two-on-One Wrist Grab (Reinforced Upward Pull)

Reach across with free hand to clasp own fist. Step back and pull both hands straight upward through the thumb gap toward your chest, following with an immediate front snap kick.

3. Green, Purple & Blue Belt: Locks & Body Escapes (Kkeokgi & Neomgigi)

Green Belt Nulleo-Kkeokgi

Single Lapel Grab (Downward Wrist Lock Counter)

Cover grabbing hand with both hands, securing opponent's knuckles and metacarpals. Step back, bend their wrist downward 90° into center chest, forcing opponent to their knees.

Purple Belt Body Ppaegi + Striking Chain

Rear Bearhug (Arms Pinned)

Drop weight rapidly into Horse Riding Stance. Deliver double heel stamp to opponent's instep, headbutt rearward to the bridge of the nose, elbow strike to ribs, turn, and execute groin strike.

Blue Belt Levering Ppaegi + Palkup-Chigi

Front Two-Hand Choke Release

Raise right arm vertically, turn shoulders 90° to break the grip leverage. Bring right forearm down forcefully across both wrists, countering with horizontal elbow strike.

4. Red Belt & 1st Dan: Hapkido Joint Locks, Chair Defenses & Sweeps

Red Belt Ogeum-Georeo-Neomgigi (Popliteal Sweep)

Side Headlock Escape & Knee-Hook Takedown

Reach over opponent's shoulder with left hand to push nose/chin backward. Strike groin with right hand, hook right leg behind opponent's knee joint (popliteal fossa), and sweep them backward to the floor.

1st Dan Black Belt Situational Neomgigi + Kkeokgi (Armbar)

Seated Chair Defense against Front Punch

From seated position, deflect incoming punch outward with knifehand block. Stand up driving palm heel under chin while kicking opponent's shin, sweeping into standing armbar control.
