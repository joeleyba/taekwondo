1. UMA Curriculum Crosswalk to Selected Kukkiwon Categories

Kukkiwon's official technical classification contains ten broad classes (punching, thrusting, kicking, striking, blocking, dodging, etc.). The following selected categories are used in this guide as conceptual crosswalks:

Ppaegi (빼기) - Joint Releases

Techniques focused on breaking an attacker's grip by rotating toward the thumb/open grip line while stepping into base.

Kkeokgi (꺾기) - Joint Locks

Joint locking and hyperextension techniques targeting the wrist, elbow, shoulder, or knee joints.

Neomgigi (넘기기) - Takedowns & Throws

Disrupting balance, sweeps, and hip throws using leverage to ground an attacker.

Chigi (치기) - Striking Techniques

Striking techniques generally (using palm heels, elbows, knees, knifehands, or backfists).

2. Belt-by-Belt Grab Defense Syllabus

White Belt Grab Defenses
  1. Same-Side Wrist Grab: Rotate wrist toward attacker's thumb, step back, and pull to hip.
  2. Cross Wrist Grab: Reach over with opposite hand, peel thumb, step back, and strike.
  3. Two-Hand on One Wrist: Form fist, grab fist with free hand, pull upward between attacker's thumbs.
Yellow Belt Grab Defenses
  1. Two-Hand Front Lapel Grab: Double upward forearm strike between arms, followed by double palm heel strike to ribs.
  2. One-Hand Lapel Grab: Pin hand with opposite palm, rotate wrist into figure-four lock, step back into lock.
  3. Rear Wrist Grabs: Step back behind attacker's lead foot, rotate arm upward, and counter with back kick.
Orange Belt Grab Defenses
  1. Front Bearhug (Arms Free): Double ear slap, grab hair/head, knee strike to groin.
  2. Front Bearhug (Arms Pinned): Headbutt, stomp on instep, drop weight, flare elbows, and hip strike.
  3. Rear Bearhug (Arms Free): Headbutt rearward, heel stomp to instep, reach behind knee to lift leg for takedown.
Green Belt Grab Defenses
  1. Front Two-Hand Choke: Bring right arm up, turn body 90° to the left, smash arm downward across attacker's forearms, follow with reverse punch.
  2. Rear Two-Hand Choke: Turn 180° raising lead arm over attacker's arms, elbow strike to ribs.
  3. Side Headlock: Reach over attacker's face, push nose/chin backward to break posture, strike groin.
Purple Belt Grab Defenses
  1. Rear Choke / Sleeper Hold: Turn chin into elbow crook to protect airway, grab attacker's elbow and wrist, drop weight and execute shoulder throw.
  2. Two-Hand Front Hair Grab: Pin attacker's hands to head with both palms, step backward bowing forward to hyperextend attacker's wrists.
Blue Belt Grab Defenses
  1. Side Shoulder Grab: Pin hand, step in, strike armpit, execute reverse wrist lock takedown.
  2. Full Nelson Defense: Drop weight into horse stance, shoot arms upward, reach behind attacker's head/neck, forward flip throw.
Brown Belt Grab Defenses
  1. Double Lapel Push: Redirect with circular palm deflection, grab wrists, execute double elbow strike.
  2. Ground Guard Defenses: Trap attacker's arm, bridge and roll (Upa) into mount position.
Black Belt Hapkido Defenses (Notes P0444–P0447 • UMA written-notes stage: Black Belt)
  1. Leg Bar / Achilles Lock: Defense against inside knee / ankle kick attack.
  2. Shoulder Throw: Defense against two-hand front choke.
  3. Arm Lock: Defense against committed cross punch.

3. Knife & Weapons Defenses

Transcribed from Master Malone's weapons self-defense notes:

  1. Overhead Knife Attack: High X-block with open hands, seize wrist with right hand, pull down into right knee strike, disarm with wrist peel.
  2. Straight Knife Thrust: Side-step evasion to outside line, left palm deflection, right ridgehand to neck, seize wrist, strip weapon toward thumb.
  3. Inverted / Backhand Knife Slash: Step back into low knifehand guard, double-hand wrist capture, twist arm into standing armbar takedown.

4. School Vital-Point Names & Proposed WHO Acupuncture Cross-Reference

Standardized acupuncture point codes and locations cross-referenced from World Health Organization (WHO) Standard Acupuncture Point Locations (ISBN: 978-9290613831). Note: Mapping school names (such as "Tang Men" and "Po Lang") to specific acupoints is an interpretive curriculum cross-reference; WHO standardizes acupuncture nomenclature and locations rather than martial striking effects or injury claims:

School Pressure Point Proposed WHO Cross-Reference Point Code Standard Anatomical Location Traditional School Martial Application
Crown Point (Baihui) Baihui (百會) GV-20 On the head, 5.0 cun superior to the anterior hairline, on the anterior midline. Downward palm/hammerfist strike causing disorientation.
Liver / Flank Point (Tang Men) Qimen (期門) LR-14 In the anterior thoracic region, in the 6th intercostal space, 4.0 cun lateral to the anterior midline. Ridgehand or round kick targeting liver/spleen.
Solar / Sternum Point (Po Lang) Shanzhong / Danzhong (膻中) CV-17 In the anterior thoracic region, on the anterior midline, at the level of the 4th intercostal space (mid-sternum). Straight punch causing respiratory disruption.
Throat Point (Tiantu) Tiantu (天突) CV-22 In the anterior region of the neck, in the center of the suprasternal fossa. Arc-hand (Kaljaebi) or fingertip thrust to trachea.
Perineum / Groin Point (Huiyin) Huiyin (會陰) CV-1 In the perineal region, at the midpoint of the line connecting the anus and the posterior border of the scrotum/vulva. Upward knee or front snap kick to groin.
Spine / Life Gate (Mingmen) Mingmen (命門) GV-4 In the lumbar region, on the posterior midline, in the depression inferior to the spinous process of the 2nd lumbar vertebra (L2). Elbow strike or back kick to lower lumbar spine.
Occiput / Back of Head (Naohu) Naohu (腦戶) GV-17 On the head, in the depression superior to the external occipital protuberance, on the posterior midline. Upward/horizontal strike to base of skull.
Mid-Spine / Thoracic (Zhiyang) Zhiyang (至陽) GV-9 In the upper back region, on the posterior midline, in the depression inferior to the spinous process of the 7th thoracic vertebra (T7). Direct palm or elbow strike to thoracic spine.
Umbilicus / Lower Abdomen (Shenque) Shenque (神闕) CV-8 In the anterior region of the abdomen, in the center of the umbilicus. Penetrating punch or front kick to abdominal wall.
