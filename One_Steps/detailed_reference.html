1. Distance Control & Stance Geometry (Encyclopedia Vol. 5)
General Principle
Middle Punch vs. Middle Block Measure In Walking Stance (*Gunnun Sogi*), middle punch knuckles align in a vertical line with the toes. To block effectively without overextending, initial distance measurement requires partners' toes to line up with the center of the partner's foot (half foot-length closer).
Ref: The Geometry of 3-Step Sparring (3step.pdf), Page 1 | Gen Choi Enc. Vol. 5, p. 110-115
High Punch vs. Rising Block Measure High punches align with the instep because the arm extends on a circle trajectory. High Rising Block (*Chukyo Makgi*) meets the attack closer to the defender's body, requiring a heel-to-heel initial measurement distance.
Ref: Step-Sparring-2017.pdf, Page 1 | Gen Choi Enc. Vol. 5, p. 120-132
Stepping Channel Geometry When stepping in Walking Stance, the attacker steps outside-inside-outside (right-right-right). In L-Stance (*Niunja Sogi*), due to narrower foot width, steps go inside-outside-inside (left-left-left).
Ref: Kildare Manual, Pages 14-18 | Gen Choi Enc. Vol. 5, p. 118
Height Adjustment Protocol If partners differ in height, the taller practitioner adopts L-Stance while the shorter practitioner uses Walking Stance to equalize stride length.
Ref: Midtown Martial Arts Handbook v14.1, Page 15
White Belt One-Step Breakdown & Page References
White Belt
  • One-Step #1: Rising Block & Reverse Punch
    Chukyo Makgi & Bandae Jirugi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Defender steps back into Left Walking Stance, executing a forearm rising block over the forehead, then shifts hip mass forward into a Right Reverse Punch targeting the solar plexus (*Myongchi*).
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 45-67 | Techniques Handbook 2017, Page 5 | Step-Sparring-2017, Page 1
  • One-Step #2: Horse Riding Stance Push Block & 3-Punch Sequence
    Annun Sogi Miul Makgi & Sambang Jirugi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Defender steps right into Sitting Stance (*Annun Sogi*), using a left palm push block to deflect the incoming punch outward, followed by immediate Low, Middle, and High punches.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 45-67 | WTSDA Hand Technique #2 (Quizlet Registry) | Techniques Handbook, Page 5
  • One-Step #3: Inner Forearm Block & Back Leg Front Kick
    Anpalmok Makgi & Ap Chagi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Defender steps back with left leg, executing a right inner forearm block (*Anpalmok Kaunde Makgi*), then delivers a powerful rear leg front snap kick using the ball of the foot (*Apkumchi*) to the solar plexus.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 45-67 | Step-Sparring-2017, Page 1 | Gen Choi Enc. Vol. 5, p. 112
  • One-Step #4: Wrist Grab & Back Leg Roundhouse Kick
    Mokjapgo & Dollyo Chagi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Defender blocks incoming punch, secures the opponent's wrist with the lead hand, and fires a rear-leg roundhouse kick using the instep (*Baldeung*) or ball of foot to the ribs/solar plexus.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 45-67 | Quizlet WTSDA Flashcard #4 | Midtown Handbook, Page 15
Yellow Belt One-Step Breakdown & Page References
Yellow Belt
  • One-Step #1: Outer Block, Wrist Grab, Front Kick & Elbow Strike
    Bakat Palmok Makgi, Ap Chagi & Palkup Taerigi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Lead outer forearm block, grab attacker's wrist to lock their distance, front snap kick to stomach, land forward into Walking Stance with a front elbow strike (*Ap Palkup Taerigi*) into the chest/chin.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 68-93 | Techniques Handbook 2017, Page 6 | Step-Sparring-2017, Page 1
  • One-Step #2: Pizza Man Defense (Simultaneous Block & Knifehand)
    Annun Sogi Sonnal Makgi & Sonnal Taerigi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Step 45° inside into Horse Riding Stance. Lead hand executes an outer forearm block while rear open palm strikes the neck artery (*Mok*) simultaneously.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 68-93 | WTSDA Hand Technique #4 ("Pizza Man") | Midtown Handbook, Page 15
  • One-Step #3: Side Kick & Knifehand Neck Strike
    Yop Chagi & Sonnal Mok Taerigi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Defender steps to 45° angle, fires a lead leg side piercing kick (*Yopcha Jirugi*) using the footsword (*Balkal*) to the ribs, steps down and executes a knifehand strike to the neck.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 68-93 | Techniques Handbook 2017, Page 6
  • One-Step #4: Reverse Inner Block, Wrist Grab, Neck Chop & Knee Strike
    Anpalmok Makgi, Sonnal Mok Taerigi & Mureup Chigi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Step inside into Sitting Stance, reverse inner forearm block with chest slap for reaction force, grab wrist, right knifehand to neck, wrap arm behind opponent's head, and drive a right knee strike into the stomach.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 68-93 | Cram TKD Flashcard Registry | Gen Choi Enc. Vol. 5, p. 250
Orange & Green Belt One-Steps & Page References
Orange & Green
  • Orange #1: X-Block, Wrist Twist, Round Kick & Leg Sweep Takedown
    Eotgollo Makgi, Kyocha & Bal Mok Sweep
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Cross-wrist X-block (*Eotgollo Makgi*), twist opponent's wrist 180° downward to break structure, roundhouse kick to midsection, and use the returning leg to sweep behind opponent's ankle for a takedown.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 94-118 | Techniques Handbook, Page 8 | Hapkido Joint Lock Manual, p. 45
  • Green #1 & #2: Crescent Kick Combinations
    Bandal Chagi & Dwi Uryu Chagi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Outside-In Crescent kick to knock down opponent's guard, followed immediately by either an opposite-leg spinning heel kick (*Dwi Uryu Chagi*) or same-leg side kick without touching the ground.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 119-142 | Step-Sparring-2017, Page 1
  • Green #4: Temple Elbow Strike & Leg Sweep Takedown
    Palkup Gwanjatnori Taerigi & Dwi Chagi Sweep
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Step outside into Sitting Stance, left outer forearm block, right elbow strike to temple (*Gwanjatnori*), slide left hand down to grab shoulder, pull left leg behind opponent's heel to execute a rear sweep takedown, finishing with a ground roundhouse kick.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 119-142 | Gen Choi Enc. Vol. 5 Self-Defense, p. 265-270
Purple, Blue & Red Belt One-Steps & Page References
Purple, Blue & Red
  • Blue #2: Low Knee Drop Round Kick & Hooking Leg Sweep
    Mureup Ground Sweep & Bandae Jirugi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Drop down onto left knee with hands anchored on the floor for balance, fire right roundhouse kick to midsection, immediately hook right leg behind opponent's forward leg to sweep them to the ground, finishing with a left reverse punch.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 164-187 | Cram TKD One-Step Flashcards | Techniques Handbook, Page 10
  • Blue #3: Wrist Fold, Front Kick & Spine Elbow
    Wrist Lock, Ap Chagi & Palkup Taerigi
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Step back into Front Stance with X-block, fold right hand over opponent's wrist in a clockwise circle (small-circle wrist lock), right front kick to face, land forward with an elbow strike to the spine.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 164-187 | Hapkido Kwan Suk Wrist Fold Manual, p. 88
  • Red #6: "Ham Sandwich" Joint Break & Rotational Takedown
    Arm Joint Break & Rotational Sweep
    Biochemistry & Alignment: Trap opponent's punching arm between lead arm and chest, apply upward hyperextension against the elbow joint, strike elbow into ribs, spin 180° around opponent's center of gravity to force a full takedown.
    Ref: Personal Notes, p. 188-217 | Midtown Martial Arts Handbook v14.1, Page 19
1st Dan Black Belt Takedowns & Page References
1st Dan Black Belt
Hapkido Leg Bar (Achilles Lock) Balmok Kkukki
From right knee, palm heel strike opponent's right inside knee while sweeping ankle with left hand. Wrap right arm around lower calf and apply small circle wrist extension driving radial bone into opponent's Achilles tendon.
Ref: Personal Notes, p. 278-305 (Hapkido #1) | Techniques Handbook, Page 18
Over-the-Shoulder Hip Throw Eo-Kkeu Deon-Ji-Gi
Against two-hand front choke: Grab right arm, shoot right arm under opponent's armpit, squat low beneath center of gravity, and throw straight over shoulder.
Ref: Personal Notes, p. 278-305 (Hapkido #2) | Midtown Handbook, Page 19
Chair & Prone Choke Defenses Eun-Ja & Laying Self-Defense
Against seated rear choke: Peel opponent's thumb, force arm over shoulder, and break elbow joint with left hand. In prone choke: Grab wrist, twist counter-clockwise, and roll body out.
Ref: Personal Notes, p. 312-321 (Exact Notes Pages 698, 699, 708)
Three-Step Sparring #3 Arm Break Sambo Matsogi Arm Lock
Rising block punch → rising block kick to groin → grab punch fist with both hands, twist 180° over shoulder to break arm over shoulder.
Ref: Personal Notes, p. 294-298 | Gen Choi Enc. Vol. 5, p. 140
