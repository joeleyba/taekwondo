1. Foundational Philosophy & Tenets (Traditional Chang-Hon / ITF)

The 5 Tenets of Taekwon-Do (태권도 5대 훈)

  • Courtesy (예의 - Ye Ui): Politeness, mutual respect, humility, and consideration for seniors, peers, and juniors.
  • Integrity (염치 - Yom Chi): Moral discernment, honesty, and knowing right from wrong with personal accountability.
  • Perseverance (인내 - In Nae): Patience, resilience, and steady effort through physical and mental hardship.
  • Self-Control (극기 - Guk Gi): Mastery over one's emotions, temper, actions, and technique inside and outside the dojang.
  • Indomitable Spirit (백절불굴 - Baekjul Boolgool): Unwavering courage and standing up for justice in the face of fear or oppression.

The Student Pledge (학생 선서)

  1. I will observe the principles and tenets of Tae Kwon Do.
  2. I will obey my instructor and all senior ranks.
  3. I will never misuse Tae Kwon Do.
  4. I will be a champion of freedom and justice.
  5. I will help to build a more peaceful world.

3. ITF Chang-Hon Stance Geometry (General Choi Encyclopedia)

Standardized stance dimensions from General Choi Hong Hi's Encyclopedia of Taekwon-Do:

Stance (ITF Name) Hangul Weight Distribution Length & Width Geometry Primary Alignment & Mechanics
Walking Stance (Gunnun Sogi) 걷는서기 50% Front / 50% Rear 1.5 shoulder widths long, 1 shoulder width wide Front knee bent over heel; rear leg straight with rear foot turned 25° outward.
L-Stance (Niunja Sogi) ㄴ어서기 70% Rear / 30% Front 1.5 shoulder widths long, 2.5 cm heel offset Both knees bent inward; feet form approximately 90° angle; weight loaded onto rear leg.
Sitting Stance (Annun Sogi) 앉는서기 50% Left / 50% Right 1.5 shoulder widths wide (big toe to big toe) Both feet parallel; knees bent outward pushing over toes; back straight.
Fixed Stance (Gojung Sogi) 고정서기 50% Front / 50% Rear 1.5 shoulder widths long, deep L-shape Deeper stance than L-stance with equal weight distribution between both feet.
Bending Ready Stance A (Guburyo Sogi A) 구부려서기 A 100% Supporting Leg Single supporting leg bent Supporting knee bent; kicking foot chambered beside knee in preparation for side kick.
Rear Foot Stance (Dwitbal Sogi) 뒷발서기 90% Rear / 10% Front 1 shoulder width long Rear knee bent; front foot rests lightly on ball of foot with heel raised.
Vertical Stance (Soojik Sogi) 수직서기 60% Rear / 40% Front 1 shoulder width long Both legs straight; body upright; feet turned 15° inward.
X-Stance (Kyocha Sogi) 교차서기 Body weight on crossing foot Legs crossed One foot crosses in front or behind the other, supporting weight on flat foot.
Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi) 나란히 준비서기 50% Left / 50% Right 1 shoulder width wide Feet parallel facing forward; fists chambered in front of abdomen.

4. Kukkiwon Standard Stance Geometry (Kukkiwon Textbook)

Standardized stance dimensions from official Kukkiwon / World Taekwondo technical standards:

Stance (Kukkiwon Name) Hangul Weight Distribution Length & Width Geometry Primary Alignment & Mechanics
Walking Stance (Ap-seogi) 앞서기 50% Front / 50% Rear Natural 1-step walking distance Upright posture, inner edges of feet parallel on walking track line.
Front Stance (Apkubi) 앞굽이 67% (2/3) Front / 33% (1/3) Rear 4.5 foot-lengths long, 1 fist-width wide Front knee bent until patella aligns over heel; rear leg straight with foot turned max 30°.
Back Stance (Dwitkubi) 뒷굽이 67% (2/3) Rear / 33% (1/3) Front 3 foot-lengths long, heels on 90° line Rear knee bent over toes; front knee bent; heels aligned on intersecting perpendicular lines.
Horse Riding Stance (Juchum-seogi) 주춤서기 50% Left / 50% Right 2 foot-lengths wide between inner edges Both feet parallel facing forward; knees flexed outward; body center lowered.
Tiger Stance (Beom-seogi) 범서기 90% Rear / 10% Front 1 foot-length distance Rear leg loaded and bent; front foot resting lightly on the ball of the foot (Apchuk).
Crossed Stance (Kkoa-seogi) 꼬아서기 Loaded on supporting flat foot Legs crossed (Dwi-kkoa / Ap-kkoa) Back crossed (Dwi-kkoa-seogi) or front crossed (Ap-kkoa-seogi) during rapid turns and transitions.
Crane Stance (Hakdari-seogi) 학다리서기 100% Supporting Leg Single supporting leg bent Supporting knee bent; opposite foot blade tucked into inner knee joint (as in Keumgang).
Pushing Ready Stance (Tongmilgi) 통밀기준비서기 50% Left / 50% Right 1 foot-length wide (Moa or Narani) Slow pushing motion with open palms at chest height (as in Koryo Poomsae).
