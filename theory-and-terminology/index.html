5 Tenets of Taekwon-Do (사훈)

1. Courtesy (예의 - Ye Ui)

Be polite, show respect to instructors and seniors, encourage justice and humanity, and practice good etiquette at all times.

2. Integrity (염치 - Yom Chi)

Distinguish right from wrong, have a clear conscience, and maintain high moral standards and self-honesty.

3. Perseverance (인내 - In Nae)

Patience and persistence. Overcome difficulties and keep striving toward goals without giving up.

4. Self-Control (극기 - Guk Gi)

Control over emotions, desires, and actions both inside and outside the dojang.

5. Indomitable Spirit (백절불굴 - Baekjul Boolgool)

Unconquerable spirit. Courage to stand up against injustice without fear or hesitation.

Student Oath / Creed (학생 선서)

  • 1. I shall observe the tenets of Taekwon-Do.
  • 2. I shall respect the instructor and seniors.
  • 3. I shall never misuse Taekwon-Do.
  • 4. I shall be a champion of freedom and justice.
  • 5. I shall build a more peaceful world.

Korean Stances & Geometry (서기 - Sogi)

  • Gunnun Sogi (Walking Stance): 1.5 shoulder widths long, 1 shoulder width wide. 50/50 weight distribution. Front leg bent, rear leg straight.
  • Niunja Sogi (L-Stance): 1.5 shoulder widths long, 2.5 cm wide. 70% weight on rear leg, 30% weight on front leg.
  • Annun Sogi (Sitting Stance): 1.5 shoulder widths wide between big toes. 50/50 weight distribution. Parallel feet pointing forward.
  • Moa Sogi (Close Stance): Feet together, big toes and heels touching. 50/50 weight distribution.
  • Gyocha Sogi (Cross Stance): One foot crosses over or behind the other, touching or nearly touching.
  • Gojung Sogi (Fixed Stance): 1.5 shoulder widths long. 50/50 weight distribution in L-stance geometry.
  • Guburyo Sogi (Bending Stance): One-legged balance stance with supporting knee slightly bent.

Target Heights & Korean Numbers (수 및 목표 높이)

Target Heights

Nopunde (High): Eye level / head level.
Kaunde (Middle): Solar plexus / chest level.
Najunde (Low): Groin / waist level and below.
