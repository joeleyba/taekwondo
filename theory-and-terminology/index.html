2. Student Creed / Oath (학생 선서)

1. I shall observe the Tenets of Taekwon-Do.
2. I shall respect instructors and seniors.
3. I shall never misuse Taekwon-Do.
4. I shall be a champion of freedom and justice.
5. I shall build a more peaceful world.

4. Stance Geometry Rules & Weight Distributions

Stance (ITF / WT) Korean Weight Distribution Dimensions & Alignment
Walking Stance (Gunnun Sogi / Apkubi) 건눈 서기 / 앞굽이 50% Front / 50% Rear 1.5 shoulder widths long, 1 shoulder width wide. Front knee bent over ankle.
L-Stance (Niunja Sogi / Dwitkubi) 니은자 서기 / 뒷굽이 70% Rear / 30% Front 1.5 shoulder widths long, feet form 90° angle (L-shape). Heel aligned with rear heel.
Sitting Stance (Annun Sogi / Juchum-Seogi) 앉은 서기 / 주춤서기 50% Left / 50% Right 1.5 shoulder widths wide, feet parallel pointing straight forward. Knees pushed outward.
Fixed Stance (Gojung Sogi) 고정 서기 50% Front / 50% Rear 1.5 shoulder widths long, weight equally distributed in deep L-configuration.
Bending Ready Stance A (Guburyo Sogi A) 구부려 준비서기 A 100% on Supporting Leg Single supporting leg bent at knee; kicking foot held beside knee joint in chamber.
Rear Foot Stance (Dwitbal Sogi) 뒷발 서기 90% Rear / 10% Front 1 shoulder width long. Front foot touches lightly on ball of foot.
X-Stance (Kyocha Sogi) 교차 서기 Weight on crossing leg Feet crossed, weight balanced on flat foot with opposite foot on ball of toes.
