WON-HYO 원효

Chang-Hon Tul Green Belt
Total Movements 28 Moves
Ready Stance Closed Ready Stance A (Moa Junbi Sogi A)
Diagram Line Capital I (Starting Line AB, Column CD, Far Line EF)
Return Foot Right Foot

Pattern Meaning

Wonhyo was an influential Korean Buddhist monk and scholar who lived from 617 to 686 AD. He helped popularize Buddhism, including Pure Land teachings, among the people of Silla.

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-6): Matching 3-movement combinations to B and A: twin forearm block, inward knife-hand strike, slide into fixed stance & punch.
  • Part 2 (Moves 7-12): Turn face to D into bending ready stance A with middle forearm guarding block, left side kick, land in three knife-hand guarding blocks and straight fingertip thrust (Kihap 12 is a school convention).
  • Part 3 (Moves 13-18): Repeat opening 3-movement combinations across line EF.
  • Part 4 (Moves 19-24): Move left foot to C with circular block to CF (19); front kick, reverse punch; move to CE circular block (22); front kick, reverse punch.
  • Part 5 (Moves 25-28): Balance in bending stance A with middle forearm guarding block, right side kick, land in forearm guarding blocks to B and A.

Part 1: Twin Forearm, Inward Knifehand & Fixed Stance Punch Combo (Moves 1 - 6)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
1 B Turn 90° Left (Move L foot to B) Right L-Stance Twin Forearm Block High
2 B Maintain Stance Right L-Stance High Inward Knife-Hand Strike (R) High
3 B Slide L foot outward to B Left Fixed Stance Middle Punch (L) Middle
4 A Bring L foot to R, Move R foot to A (Turn 180°) Left L-Stance Twin Forearm Block High
5 A Maintain Stance Left L-Stance High Inward Knife-Hand Strike (L) High
6 A Slide R foot outward to A Right Fixed Stance Middle Punch (R) Middle
Part 2: Bending Stance Side Kick, 3 Knifehand Blocks & Fingertip Thrust (Moves 7 - 12)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
7 D Turn face toward D while forming Right Bending Ready Stance A Right Bending Ready Stance A Middle Forearm Guarding Block (L) in Bending Stance Middle
8 D Kick to D Kicking Position Middle Side Piercing Kick (L) Middle
9 D Lower L foot to D Right L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
10 D Step forward (Move R foot to D) Left L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
11 D Step forward (Move L foot to D) Right L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
12 D Step forward (Move R foot to D) Right Walking Middle Straight Fingertip Thrust (R) KIHAP! Middle
Part 3: Twin Forearm Block Combo across Line EF (Moves 13 - 18)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
13 E Turn 270° Left to Face E (Move L foot to E) Right L-Stance Twin Forearm Block High
14 E Maintain Stance Right L-Stance High Inward Knife-Hand Strike (R) High
15 E Slide L foot outward to E Left Fixed Stance Middle Punch (L) Middle
16 F Bring L foot to R, Move R foot to F (Turn 180°) Left L-Stance Twin Forearm Block High
17 F Maintain Stance Left L-Stance High Inward Knife-Hand Strike (L) High
18 F Slide R foot outward to F Right Fixed Stance Middle Punch (R) Middle
Part 4: Circular Blocks, Front Kicks & Reverse Punches Facing Back (Moves 19 - 24)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
19 C Move L foot to C into Left Walking Stance Left Walking Right Inner-Forearm Circular Block to CF Middle
20 C Kick to C Kicking Position Low Front Snap Kick (R) Low
21 C Lower R foot to C Right Walking Middle Reverse Punch (L) Middle
22 C Maintain Right Walking Stance Right Walking Left Inner-Forearm Circular Block to CE Middle
23 C Kick to C Kicking Position Low Front Snap Kick (L) Low
24 C Lower L foot to C Left Walking Middle Reverse Punch (R) Middle
Part 5: Bending Stance Side Kick & Forearm Guarding Blocks (Moves 25 - 28)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
25 C Turn face toward C while forming Left Bending Ready Stance A Left Bending Ready Stance A Middle Forearm Guarding Block (R) in Bending Stance Middle
26 C Kick to C Kicking Position Middle Side Piercing Kick (R) Middle
27 B Lower R foot on line CD, Move L foot to B Right L-Stance Middle Forearm Guarding Block Middle
28 A Bring L foot to R, Move R foot to A Left L-Stance Middle Forearm Guarding Block Middle
