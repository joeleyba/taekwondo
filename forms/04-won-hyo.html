WON-HYO 원효

Green Belt
Total Movements 28 Moves
Ready Stance Closed Ready Stance A (Moa Junbi Sogi A)
Diagram Line Capital I (Upper Crossbar AB, Column CD, Lower Crossbar EF)
Return Foot Right Foot

Pattern Meaning

Wonhyo was an influential Korean Buddhist monk and scholar who lived from 617 to 686 AD. He helped popularize Buddhism, including Pure Land teachings, among the people of Silla.

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-6): Matching 3-movement combinations to B and A: twin forearm block, inward knife-hand strike, slide into fixed stance & punch.
  • Part 2 (Moves 7-12): Balance in bending ready stance A, left side kick, land in three knife-hand guarding blocks and straight spear-hand thrust (Kihap 12).
  • Part 3 (Moves 13-18): Repeat opening 3-movement combinations across lower crossbar lines E and F.
  • Part 4 (Moves 19-24): Two circular-block combinations facing back: inner forearm circular block, low front kick, and reverse punch.
  • Part 5 (Moves 25-28): Balance in bending stance A, right side kick, land in forearm guarding blocks to B and A.
