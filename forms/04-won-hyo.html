WON-HYO (원효)

28 Movements | Ready Stance: Closed Ready Stance A (Moa Junbi Sogi A)
Green Belt Form #4

Pattern Meaning: Won-Hyo was the noted monk (617-686 AD) who introduced and popularized Pure Land Buddhism to the Silla Dynasty.

Return Foot: Right Foot returns to Ready Stance.

Key Form Structure & Memory Anchors:
  • Wonhyo lifespan: 617–686 AD.
  • Combo (Moves 1-3 & 4-6): Twin Forearm Block -> Inward Knifehand -> Middle Punch.
  • Moves 19-21 & 22-24: Forearm Circular Block -> Low Front Snap Kick -> Middle Reverse Punch.
