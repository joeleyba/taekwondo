DO-SAN (도산)

24 Movements | Ready Stance: Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Orange Belt Form #3

Pattern Meaning: Do-San is the pseudonym of patriot Ahn Chang-Ho (1878-1938), who devoted his life to furthering education in Korea and its independence movement.

Return Foot: Right Foot returns to Ready Stance.

Key Form Structure & Memory Anchors:
  • Ahn Chang-ho lifespan: 1878–1938.
  • Move 6: Straight Fingertip Thrust to D, followed by releasing twist (Move 7).
  • Moves 13-16 and 17-20: Wedging Block -> Front Snap Kick -> Fast Motion Double Punch to CE & CF.
