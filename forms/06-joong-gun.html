JOONG-GUN 중근

Chang-Hon Tul Blue Belt
Total Movements 32 Moves
Ready Stance Closed Ready Stance B (Moa Junbi Sogi B)
Diagram Line Capital I (Starting Line AB, Column CD, Far Line EF)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Named after Korean independence activist An Jung-geun (commonly written Ahn Joong-Gun in Taekwon-Do), who assassinated Itō Hirobumi, Japan's first Resident-General of Korea in 1909. An was executed in 1910; the 32 movements represent his age under traditional Korean age counting (30 by modern reckoning).

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-6): Mirrored combinations of reverse knife-hand block, same-side low kick, and upward palm block in rear-foot stance.
  • Part 2 (Moves 7-13): Two knife-hand guarding blocks with upper-elbow strikes down column CD, twin vertical & twin upset punches (Kihap 12 is a school convention), turning back with high X-fist rising block.
  • Part 3 (Moves 14-19): Side lines E & F: high backfist, wrist release (rotating backfist down / palm up) & high reverse punch in fast motion.
  • Part 4 (Moves 20-26): Column CD back: close feet then step L to C double-forearm block, pull into L-stance & punch, middle side kick, repeated on opposite side.
  • Part 5 (Moves 27-32): Slow low-stance pressing block, forearm guard, slow low-stance pressing block, slow angle punch in close stance, and U-shaped blocks to A and B.

Part 1: Mirrored Opening Combinations (Moves 1 - 6)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
1 B Step L foot to B (Turn 90° Left) Right L-Stance Reverse Knife-Hand Middle Side Block (L) Middle
2 B Kick to B (Same leg) Kicking Position Low Side-Front Snap Kick (L) Low
3 B Lower L foot, step R foot to B Left Rear Foot Stance Upward Palm Block (R) Middle
4 A Step R foot to A (Turn 180° Clockwise) Left L-Stance Reverse Knife-Hand Middle Side Block (R) Middle
5 A Kick to A (Same leg) Kicking Position Low Side-Front Snap Kick (R) Low
6 A Lower R foot, step L foot to A Right Rear Foot Stance Upward Palm Block (L) Middle
Part 2: Guarding Blocks, Elbow Strikes & Twin Punches down Column CD (Moves 7 - 13)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
7 D Turn 90° Left to Face Front (Move L foot to D) Right L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
8 D Slip L foot forward into Left Walking Stance Left Walking Right Upper Elbow Strike High
9 D Step R foot forward to D Left L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
10 D Slip R foot forward into Right Walking Stance Right Walking Left Upper Elbow Strike High
11 D Step L foot forward to D Left Walking High Twin Vertical Punch High
12 D Step R foot forward to D Right Walking Middle Twin Upset Punch KIHAP! Middle
13 C Move R foot onto line CD, turn 180° Left to C Left Walking High X-Fist Rising Block (Kyocha Joomuk) High
Part 3: Backfist, Wrist Release & High Reverse Punch in Fast Motion (Moves 14 - 19)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
14 E Turn 90° Left to Face E (Step L foot) Right L-Stance High Backfist Side Strike (L) High
15 E Twist L fist until backfist faces downward (palm up) & slip L foot (Fast Motion) Left Walking Wrist Release Motion Middle
16 E Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Left Walking High Reverse Punch (R) High
17 F Bring L foot to R foot, then move R foot to F (Turn 180° Clockwise to Face F) Left L-Stance High Backfist Side Strike (R) High
18 F Twist R fist until backfist faces downward (palm up) & slip R foot (Fast Motion) Right Walking Wrist Release Motion Middle
19 F Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Right Walking High Reverse Punch (L) High
Part 4: Double Forearm Block, Pull Punch & Side Kick to C (Moves 20 - 26)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
20 C Bring R foot to L foot, then move L foot to C (Turn 90° Left to Face Back) Left Walking High Double Forearm Block High
21 C Pull L foot back into Right L-Stance Right L-Stance Middle Obverse Punch (L) Middle
22 C Kick to C Kicking Position Middle Side Piercing Kick (R) Middle
23 C Lower R foot forward to C Right Walking High Double Forearm Block High
24 C Pull R foot back into Left L-Stance Left L-Stance Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
25 C Kick to C Kicking Position Middle Side Piercing Kick (L) Middle
26 C Lower L foot forward to C Right L-Stance Middle Forearm Guarding Block Middle
Part 5: Slow Pressing Blocks, Slow Angle Punch & U-Shape Blocks Finish (Moves 27 - 32)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
27 C Slip L foot forward to C Left Low Stance Right Palm Pressing Block (Slow Motion) Low
28 C Step R foot forward to C Left L-Stance Middle Forearm Guarding Block Middle
29 C Slip R foot forward to C Right Low Stance Left Palm Pressing Block (Slow Motion) Low
30 A Bring L foot to R foot (Facing A) Close Stance Right Middle Angle Punch (Slow Motion) Middle
31 A Step R foot to A Right Fixed Stance Right U-Shape Block -
32 B Bring R foot to L, Step L foot to B Left Fixed Stance Left U-Shape Block -
