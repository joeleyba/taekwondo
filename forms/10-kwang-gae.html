KWANG-GAE 광개

1st Dan Black Belt • Chang-Hon Form
Total Movements 39 Moves
Ready Stance Parallel Stance with Heaven Hand (Hanulson / 하늘손 / Narani Junbi Sogi)
Diagram Line Plus Sign (+)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Kwang-Gae is named after Gwanggaeto the Great, the nineteenth king of Goguryeo. Beginning his reign in 391 AD, he expanded Goguryeo to its greatest territorial extent. The pattern has 39 movements, referring to the first two numbers of 391. Its plus-shaped diagram (+) represents the expansion and recovery of territory.

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-12): Heaven Hand closes into ready stance B; two slow opposite-hand upset punches (2-3), right/left palm hooks & low knifehands (4-7), two rear-foot knifehand guards (8-9), two slow opposite-palm upward blocks (10-11), and circular low knifehand block (12).
  • Part 2 (Moves 13-22): Left consecutive kicks to E (13-14), inward knifehand & downward side-fist; right consecutive kicks to F (17-18), inward knifehand & downward side-fist; two slow opposite-palm pressing blocks (21-22).
  • Part 3 (Moves 23-30): Stamping backfist and Kihap (23), double forearm, reverse low block, slow fingertip thrust; mirrored on opposite side with stamping backfist and Kihap (27).
  • Part 4 (Moves 31-39): Twin vertical punch down the stem (31), followed by mirrored upset-punch, front-kick, knifehand-guard, and high-punch combinations across crossbars A and B (32-35 & 36-39).
