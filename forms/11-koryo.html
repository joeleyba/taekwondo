KORYO POOMSAE 고려 품새

1st Dan Black Belt (Kukkiwon)
Total Movements 30 Moves
Ready Stance Tongmilgi Junbi-Seogi (Pushing Hands Ready Stance)
Diagram Line Chinese Character 士 (Scholar)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Koryo represents the seonbae: a learned person of upright character who combines scholarly discipline with a strong martial spirit. Kukkiwon connects this lineage through Goguryeo, Balhae, and Goryeo. The floor pattern traces 士, the character for scholar.

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-8): Mirrored opening side lines: assisted knifehand block, repeated side kicks (low/high) landing with outward knifehand strike, reverse punch, and inward block to B and A.
  • Part 2 (Moves 9-15): Advancing center stem: low knifehand blocks with arc-hand throat strikes (Kihap 11), front kicks, knee-breaking joint locks, and double forearm opening blocks.
  • Part 3 (Moves 16-26): Distinctive side-arm sequence: knifehand side block in horse stance, target punch into palm, cross-step side kick, low upset fingertip thrust, pressing block, and supported elbow strike; mirrored on opposite side; closing with slow hammerfist into palm.
  • Part 4 (Moves 27-30): Finishing stem: alternating outward/neck knifehand strikes with low knifehand blocks, finishing with right arc-hand throat strike (Kihap 30) and return to Tongmilgi ready stance.

8 A Draw L foot back into Right Dwitkubi Right Dwitkubi (Back Stance) Momtong An-Makki (Left Inward Middle Block) Middle
