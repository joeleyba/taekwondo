KORYO POOMSAE 고려 품새

Kukkiwon Yudanja Poomsae Black Belt (Kukkiwon 1st Dan)
Total Movements 30 Moves
Ready Stance Tongmilgi Junbi-Seogi (Pushing Hands Ready Posture in Naranhi-seogi)
Diagram Line Chinese Character 士 (Scholar)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Koryo represents the seonbae: a learned person of upright character who combines scholarly discipline with a strong martial spirit. Kukkiwon connects this lineage through Goguryeo, Balhae, and Goryeo. The floor pattern traces 士, the character for scholar.

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-8): Mirrored opening side lines: knifehand guarding blocks, repeated side kicks (low/high) landing with outward knifehand strike, reverse punch, and inward block to B (Oreun Dwitkubi) and A (Wen Dwitkubi).
  • Part 2 (Moves 9-15): Advancing center stem: low knifehand blocks with arc-hand throat strikes (Kihap 11), front kicks, knee pressing and lock techniques (Mureup-nulleo-kkeokgi), and double inner-forearm opening blocks.
  • Part 3 (Moves 16-26): Distinctive side-arm sequence: knifehand side block in horse stance, target punch into palm, cross-step side kick, low upset flat-fingertip thrust, pressing block, and target elbow strike; mirrored on opposite side; closing with slow low hammerfist into palm in Moa-seogi (26).
  • Part 4 (Moves 27-30): Finishing stem: alternating outward/neck inward knifehand strikes then low knifehand blocks, finishing with right arc-hand throat strike (Kihap 30) and return to Tongmilgi ready stance.

Part 1: Knifehand Guarding Blocks, Repeated Side Kicks & Reverse Punches (Moves 1 - 8)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
1 B Turn 90° Left (Move L foot to B) Oreun Dwitkubi (Right Back Stance) Sonnal Momtong-Makgi (Knife-Hand Guarding Middle Block) Middle
2 B Low Side Kick then High Side Kick (R foot), then land to B Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Geodeup-Yeop-Chagi, then Sonnal Bakkat-Chigi (R Outward Knife-Hand Strike) High
3 B Maintain Stance Right Apkubi Baro-Jireugi (Left Middle Reverse Punch) Middle
4 B Draw R foot back into Left Dwitkubi Wen Dwitkubi (Left Back Stance) Momtong An-Makgi (Right Inward Middle Block) Middle
5 A Turn 180° Clockwise (Move R foot to A) Wen Dwitkubi (Left Back Stance) Sonnal Momtong-Makgi (Knife-Hand Guarding Middle Block) Middle
6 A Low Side Kick then High Side Kick (L foot), then land to A Left Apkubi (Front Stance) Geodeup-Yeop-Chagi, then Sonnal Bakkat-Chigi (L Outward Knife-Hand Strike) High
7 A Maintain Stance Left Apkubi Baro-Jireugi (Right Middle Reverse Punch) Middle
8 A Draw L foot back into Right Dwitkubi Oreun Dwitkubi (Right Back Stance) Momtong An-Makgi (Left Inward Middle Block) Middle
Part 2: Arc-Hand Strikes, Front Kicks, Knee Press-Locks & Opening Blocks (Moves 9 - 15)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
9 D Turn 90° Left to Face Front (Move L foot to D) Left Apkubi (Front Stance) Han-Sonnal Arae-Makgi (L Low Knife-Hand) & Kaljaebi (R Arc-Hand Strike) High/Low
10 D Front Snap Kick (R foot), land forward to D Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Han-Sonnal Arae-Makgi (R Low Knife-Hand) & Kaljaebi (L Arc-Hand Strike) High/Low
11 D Front Snap Kick (L foot), land forward to D Left Apkubi (Front Stance) Han-Sonnal Arae-Makgi (L Low Knife-Hand) & Kaljaebi (R Arc-Hand Strike) KIHAP! High/Low
12 D Front Snap Kick (R foot), land forward to D Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Mureup-Nulleo-Kkeokgi (Knee Pressing and Lock Technique) Middle
13 C Step L foot forward, turn rightward 180° into Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Momtong Hecheo-Makgi (Double Inner-Forearm Opening Block) Middle
14 C Front Snap Kick (L foot), land forward to C Left Apkubi (Front Stance) Mureup-Nulleo-Kkeokgi (Knee Pressing and Lock Technique) Middle
15 C Draw L foot back into Left Walking Stance Left Ap-Seogi (Walking Stance) Momtong Hecheo-Makgi (Double Inner-Forearm Opening Block) Middle
Part 3: Horse Stance Target Punches, Side Kicks, Elbow Strikes & Hammerfist (Moves 16 - 26)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
16 B Turn 90° Left into Horse Stance (facing D) Juchum-Seogi (Horse Stance) Han-Sonnal Momtong Yeop-Makgi (Left Knife-Hand Side Block) Middle
17 B Maintain Horse Stance Juchum-Seogi Pyojeok-Jireugi (Right Target Punch into Left Open Palm) Middle
18 B Cross-step R behind L, Left Side Kick, turn & land to B Left Apkubi (Front Stance) Pyeonsonkkeut Jeocheo-Jjireugi (Low Upset Flat-Fingertip Thrust) Low
19 B Draw L foot back into Left Walking Stance Left Ap-Seogi (Walking Stance) Right Arae-Makgi (Right Low Forearm Block) Low
20 B Left Palm Pressing Block, advance R foot into Horse Stance Juchum-Seogi (facing D) Palgup Pyojeok-Chigi (Supported Right Target Elbow Strike into Left Palm) Middle
21 A Maintain Horse Stance (facing D) Juchum-Seogi Han-Sonnal Momtong Yeop-Makgi (Right Knife-Hand Side Block) Middle
22 A Maintain Horse Stance Juchum-Seogi Pyojeok-Jireugi (Left Target Punch into Right Open Palm) Middle
23 A Cross-step L behind R, Right Side Kick, turn & land to A Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Pyeonsonkkeut Jeocheo-Jjireugi (Low Upset Flat-Fingertip Thrust) Low
24 A Draw R foot back into Right Walking Stance Right Ap-Seogi (Walking Stance) Left Arae-Makgi (Left Low Forearm Block) Low
25 A Right Palm Pressing Block, advance L foot into Horse Stance Juchum-Seogi (facing D) Palgup Pyojeok-Chigi (Supported Left Target Elbow Strike into Right Palm) Middle
26 D Bring L foot to R foot in Closed Stance (Slow Motion) Moa-Seogi (Closed Stance) Wen-Mejumeok Arae Pyojeok-Chigi (Slow Left Hammerfist Target Strike into Right Palm) Low
Part 4: Alternating Inward Knifehand Strikes, Low Blocks & Final Arc-Hand (Moves 27 - 30)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
27 B Turn 90° Left (Move L foot to B) Left Apkubi (Front Stance) Left Sonnal Bakkat-Chigi (Outward Strike), then Left Arae-Makgi (Low Block) High/Low
28 B Step forward (Move R foot to B) Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Right Sonnal An-Chigi (Inward Strike to Neck), then Right Arae-Makgi (Low Block) High/Low
29 B Step forward (Move L foot to B) Left Apkubi (Front Stance) Left Sonnal An-Chigi (Inward Strike to Neck), then Left Arae-Makgi (Low Block) High/Low
30 B Step forward (Move R foot to B) Right Apkubi (Front Stance) Kaljaebi / Agwison (Right Arc-Hand Throat Strike) KIHAP! High
