CHON-JI 천지

Chang-Hon Tul White Belt
Total Movements 19 Moves
Ready Stance Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Diagram Line Cross +
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

According to the Chang-Hon/General Choi tradition, Chon-Ji literally means 'Heaven and Earth'. In the Orient, it is interpreted as the creation of the world or the beginning of human history, performed by the beginner. Consists of two similar parts (Heaven & Earth).

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-8): Four low-block and punch pairs around the cross.
  • Part 2 (Moves 9-16): Four inner-block and punch pairs around the cross.
  • Part 3 (Moves 17-19): Finish with three punches facing front: forward once, backward twice.
  • Left Foot steps forward to return to Parallel Ready Stance. (Kihap on Move 19 is a school/instructor convention; not specified in General Choi text).

Part 1: Low Blocks & Punches (Moves 1 - 8)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
1 B Turn 90° Left (Move L foot to B) Left Walking Low Outer Forearm Block (L) Low
2 B Step forward (Move R foot to B) Right Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (R) Middle
3 A Turn 180° Clockwise (Move R foot to A) Right Walking Low Outer Forearm Block (R) Low
4 A Step forward (Move L foot to A) Left Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (L) Middle
5 D Turn 90° Left to Face Front (Move L foot to D) Left Walking Low Outer Forearm Block (L) Low
6 D Step forward (Move R foot to D) Right Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (R) Middle
7 C Turn 180° Clockwise to Face Back (Move R foot to C) Right Walking Low Outer Forearm Block (R) Low
8 C Step forward (Move L foot to C) Left Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (L) Middle
Part 2: Inner Blocks & Punches (Moves 9 - 16)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
9 A Turn 90° Left to Face Original Right (Move L foot to A) Right L-Stance Middle Inner Forearm Block (L) Middle
10 A Step forward (Move R foot to A) Right Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (R) Middle
11 B Turn 180° Clockwise to Face Original Left (Move R foot to B) Left L-Stance Middle Inner Forearm Block (R) Middle
12 B Step forward (Move L foot to B) Left Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (L) Middle
13 C Turn 90° Left to Face Back (Move L foot to C) Right L-Stance Middle Inner Forearm Block (L) Middle
14 C Step forward (Move R foot to C) Right Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (R) Middle
15 D Turn 180° Clockwise to Face Front (Move R foot to D) Left L-Stance Middle Inner Forearm Block (R) Middle
16 D Step forward (Move L foot to D) Left Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (L) Middle
Part 3: Three-Punch Finish & Return (Moves 17 - 19)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
17 D Step forward facing Front (Move R foot to D) Right Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (R) Middle
18 D Step backward facing Front (Move R foot back to C) Left Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (L) Middle
19 D Step backward facing Front (Move L foot back to C) Right Walking Middle Obverse Forefist Punch (R) KIHAP! Middle
