CHON-JI (천지)

19 Movements | Ready Stance: Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
White Belt Form #1

Pattern Meaning: Chon-Ji literally means 'Heaven and Earth'. In the Orient, it is interpreted as the creation of the world or the beginning of human history, performed by the beginner. Consists of two similar parts (Heaven & Earth).

Return Foot: Left Foot returns to Ready Stance.

Key Form Structure & Memory Anchors:
  • Part 1 (Moves 1-8): Low Outer Forearm Block -> Middle Punch (All Walking Stances).
  • Part 2 (Moves 9-19): Middle Inner Forearm Block (L-Stance) -> Middle Punch (Walking Stance).
  • Left Foot returns to ready stance (Moa/Narani Junbi Sogi) after Move 19.
