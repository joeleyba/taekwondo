DAN-GUN 단군

Chang-Hon Tul Yellow Belt
Total Movements 21 Moves
Ready Stance Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Diagram Line Capital I (Starting Line AB, Column CD, Far Line EF)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Named after Dangun Wanggeom, the legendary founder of Gojoseon according to Korean foundational tradition (dated to 2333 BCE).

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-4): Two knife-hand guarding blocks with high punches across starting line AB.
  • Part 2 (Moves 5-8): Low block, then three high marching punches forward down column CD (Kihap 8 is a school/instructor convention).
  • Part 3 (Moves 9-12): Two twin-forearm blocks with high punches to E and F across far line EF.
  • Part 4 (Moves 13-17): Continuous low block into rising block, followed by three more rising blocks facing back (C) (Kihap 17 is a school/instructor convention).
  • Part 5 (Moves 18-21): Two outward knife-hand strikes with high punches to B and A. Every punch in Dan-Gun is high.
Part 1: Guarding Blocks & High Punches (Moves 1 - 4)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
1 B Turn 90° Left (Move L foot to B) Right L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
2 B Step forward (Move R foot to B) Right Walking High Obverse Punch (R) High
3 A Turn 180° Clockwise (Move R foot to A) Left L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
4 A Step forward (Move L foot to A) Left Walking High Obverse Punch (L) High
Part 2: Low Block & Three Marching High Punches Forward (Moves 5 - 8)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
5 D Turn 90° Left to Face Front (Move L foot to D) Left Walking Low Outer Forearm Block (L) Low
6 D Step forward (Move R foot to D) Right Walking High Obverse Punch (R) High
7 D Step forward (Move L foot to D) Left Walking High Obverse Punch (L) High
8 D Step forward (Move R foot to D) Right Walking High Obverse Punch (R) KIHAP! High
Part 3: Twin Forearm Blocks & High Punches across Far Line E & F (Moves 9 - 12)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
9 E Turn 270° Left to Face E (Move L foot to E) Right L-Stance Twin Forearm Block High
10 E Step forward (Move R foot to E) Right Walking High Obverse Punch (R) High
11 F Turn 180° Clockwise to Face F (Move R foot to F) Left L-Stance Twin Forearm Block High
12 F Step forward (Move L foot to F) Left Walking High Obverse Punch (L) High
Part 4: Continuous Low-to-Rising Block & Three Rising Blocks Facing Back (Moves 13 - 17)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
13 C Turn 90° Left to Face Back (Move L foot to C) Left Walking Low Outer Forearm Block (L) Low
14 C Continuous Motion (Maintain stance) Left Walking High Rising Block (L) High
15 C Step forward (Move R foot to C) Right Walking High Rising Block (R) High
16 C Step forward (Move L foot to C) Left Walking High Rising Block (L) High
17 C Step forward (Move R foot to C) Right Walking High Rising Block (R) KIHAP! High
Part 5: Outward Knifehand Strikes & High Punches Finish (Moves 18 - 21)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
18 B Turn 270° Left to Face B (Move L foot to B) Right L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Outward Strike (L) Middle
19 B Step forward (Move R foot to B) Right Walking High Obverse Punch (R) High
20 A Turn 180° Clockwise to Face A (Move R foot to A) Left L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Outward Strike (R) Middle
21 A Step forward (Move L foot to A) Left Walking High Obverse Punch (L) High
