CHOONG-MOO 충무

Chang-Hon Tul Brown Belt Black Stripe (Kup number: instructor confirmation)
Total Movements 30 Moves
Ready Stance Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Diagram Line Capital I (Line AB and Column CD)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Choong-Moo was the posthumous title of Admiral Yi Sun-sin of Korea's Joseon Dynasty. He redesigned, revived, and deployed the armored turtle ship (Geobukseon) during the Japanese invasions beginning in 1592. The pattern ends with a left-hand attack, traditionally symbolizing his death before he could demonstrate his full potential, constrained by his loyalty to the king.

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-8): Twin knifehands, high knifehand strike, guard, flat fingertip thrust, guard, balance posture, side kick, and guard.
  • Part 2 (Moves 9-13): Flying side kick and Kihap (9 - Choi primary), low block, head grab, upward knee strike, and reverse knifehand strike.
  • Part 3 (Moves 14-17): High turning kick & back kick in fast motion (14-15), forearm guard, and turning kick.
  • Part 4 (Moves 18-22): Fixed U-shape block, jump 360° spin and Kihap (19 - Choi primary), low upset fingertip thrust, backfist with low block, and straight fingertip thrust.
  • Part 5 (Moves 23-30): Double-forearm block, forearm block then backfist, two side kicks, X-knifehand checking block, twin upward palm block, rising block, and left reverse punch.
