CHOONG-MOO 충무

1st Kup (Senior Red / Black Stripe)
Total Movements 30 Moves
Ready Stance Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Diagram Line Capital I (Line AB and Column CD)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Named after Admiral Yi Sun-sin of the Joseon Dynasty, credited with inventing the armored Turtle Ship (Geobukseon) in 1592. The pattern ends with a left-handed strike to symbolize his untimely death in battle.

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-8): Knife-hand guard to B, chop & forehead guard, knife-hand guard to A, flat fingertip thrust, knife-hand guard to D, bending stance, side kick, and knife-hand guard.
  • Part 2 (Moves 9-13): Flying side piercing kick (9), low forearm block to E, head grab & upward knee kick (11-12), and reverse knife-hand high strike to F (13).
  • Part 3 (Moves 14-17): High turning kick & middle back kick in fast motion (14-15), forearm guarding block to E (16), and middle turning kick to DE (17).
  • Part 4 (Moves 18-22): Fixed stance U-shape block to C (18), jump 360° spin landing in knife-hand guard (Kihap 19), low upset fingertip thrust (20), side backfist & low block (21), and straight fingertip thrust (22).
  • Part 5 (Moves 23-30): High double forearm block to B (23), sitting stance front block & backfist to B (24), consecutive side kicks to A (25-26), X-knifehand checking block (27), twin upward palm block (28), right rising block (29), and left middle punch to A (30).
