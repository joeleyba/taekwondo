TOI-GYE 퇴계

Chang-Hon Tul Red Belt
Total Movements 37 Moves
Ready Stance Closed Ready Stance B (Moa Junbi Sogi B)
Diagram Line Scholar Symbol 士
Return Foot Right Foot

Pattern Meaning

Toi-Gye was the pen name of Yi Hwang, a prominent Korean Neo-Confucian scholar who lived from 1501 to 1570. The 37 movements refer to his birthplace near the 37th parallel, and the floor pattern represents the Chinese character for scholar (士).

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-6): Mirrored inner forearm blocks, low upside-down fingertip thrusts, and slow-motion side-back strikes to C while extending companion arm side-downward.
  • Part 2 (Moves 7-12): Continuous low X-fist pressing block into high twin vertical punch in left walking stance (7-8), front kick, two punches, and slow twin side-elbow thrust.
  • Part 3 (Moves 13-18): Six stamping W-shape blocks in sitting stance forming the central scholar symbol 士 line.
  • Part 4 (Moves 19-30): Bring R foot to L then step L to D in left low double forearm pushing block (19), head grab & upward knee kick (20-21), step C knifehand guard, two front kick & fingertip thrust combos to C (23-27), backfist to D in L-stance facing C (28), jump to C into right X-stance facing A (Kihap 29), and double forearm block to C (30).
  • Part 5 (Moves 31-37): Low knifehand guards to B and A (Move 33 brings L foot to R then steps R to A), circular blocks (32 to BD, 34 to AD, 35 to CE, 36 circular block), finishing with sitting stance right punch facing D on line AB (37).

Part 1: Inner Block, Fingertip Thrust & Slow Side-Back Strike (Moves 1 - 6)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
1 B Step L foot to B (Turn 90° Left) Right L-Stance Middle Inner Forearm Block (L) Middle
2 B Slip L foot forward to B Left Walking Low Upside-Down Fingertip Thrust (R) Low
3 D Bring L foot to R foot (Facing D) Close Stance Right Backfist Side-Back Strike to C while extending Left Arm Side-Downward (Slow Motion) High
4 A Step R foot to A (Turn 180° Clockwise) Left L-Stance Middle Inner Forearm Block (R) Middle
5 A Slip R foot forward to A Right Walking Low Upside-Down Fingertip Thrust (L) Low
6 D Bring R foot to L foot (Facing D) Close Stance Left Backfist Side-Back Strike to C while extending Right Arm Side-Downward (Slow Motion) High
Part 2: Continuous X-Block & Twin Punch, Front Kick & Slow Twin Elbow Thrust (Moves 7 - 12)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
7 D Turn 90° Left to Face Front (Move L foot to D) Left Walking Low X-Fist Pressing Block Low
8 D Continuous Motion (Maintain Left Walking Stance) Left Walking High Twin Vertical Punch High
9 D Kick to D Kicking Position Middle Front Snap Kick (R) Middle
10 D Lower R foot forward to D Right Walking Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
11 D Maintain Stance Right Walking Middle Reverse Punch (L) Middle
12 F Bring L foot to R foot (Facing F) Close Stance Twin Side-Elbow Thrust (Slow Motion) Middle
Part 3: Six Stamping W-Shape Blocks in Sitting Stance (Moves 13 - 18)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
13 F Move R foot to F in a Stamping Motion (1st stance F-ward) Sitting Stance (facing C) Right Outer Forearm W-Shape Block High
14 F Move L foot to F in a Stamping Motion (2nd stance F-ward, turn 180° Clockwise) Sitting Stance (facing D) Left Outer Forearm W-Shape Block High
15 E Move L foot to E in a Stamping Motion (1st stance E-ward, turn 180° Clockwise) Sitting Stance (facing C) Left Outer Forearm W-Shape Block High
16 E Move R foot to E in a Stamping Motion (2nd stance E-ward, turn 180° Counter-Clockwise) Sitting Stance (facing D) Right Outer Forearm W-Shape Block High
17 E Move L foot to E in a Stamping Motion (returning toward E, turn 180° Clockwise) Sitting Stance (facing C) Left Outer Forearm W-Shape Block High
18 F Move L foot to F in a Stamping Motion (returning toward F, turn 180° Clockwise) Sitting Stance (facing D) Left Outer Forearm W-Shape Block High
Part 4: Knee Kick, Front Kicks to C, Backfist & Jump X-Fist (Moves 19 - 30)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
19 D Bring R foot to L, then move L foot to D Right L-Stance Low Double Forearm Pushing Block (L) Low
20 D Slip L foot forward Left Walking Head Grab Motion (Both Hands Upward) High
21 D Kick to D & Pull Hands Downward Kicking Position Upward Knee Kick (R) High
22 C Lower R foot to L foot, Move L foot toward C Right L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
23 C Kick toward C Kicking Position Low Side-Front Snap Kick (L) Low
24 C Lower L foot forward toward C Left Walking High Flat Fingertip Thrust (L) High
25 C Step R foot forward toward C Left L-Stance Middle Knife-Hand Guarding Block Middle
26 C Kick toward C Kicking Position Low Side-Front Snap Kick (R) Low
27 C Lower R foot forward toward C Right Walking High Flat Fingertip Thrust (R) High
28 C Move R foot toward D, forming Right L-Stance toward C Right L-Stance (facing C) Right Backfist Side-Back Strike (D) & Left Low Block (C) High/Low
29 C Jump to C, landing in Right X-Stance toward A Right X-Stance (facing A) Low X-Fist Pressing Block KIHAP! Low
30 C Move R foot toward C Right Walking High Double Forearm Block High
Part 5: Low Knifehand Guards, Circular Blocks & Sitting Punch Finish (Moves 31 - 37)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
31 B Move L foot to B (Turn 90° Left) Right L-Stance Low Knife-Hand Guarding Block Low
32 BD Slip L foot forward toward BD Left Walking Middle Inner Forearm Circular Block (R) Middle
33 A Bring L foot to R foot, then move R foot to A (Turn 180° Clockwise) Left L-Stance (facing A) Low Knife-Hand Guarding Block Low
34 AD Slip R foot forward toward AD Right Walking Middle Inner Forearm Circular Block (L) Middle
35 CE Step L foot toward C (facing CE) Left Walking Middle Inner Forearm Circular Block (R) Middle
36 A Move R foot toward A into Right Walking Stance Right Walking Middle Inner Forearm Circular Block (L) [Published sources differ between CE and AD; confirm with instructor] Middle
37 D Move R foot onto line AB, form Sitting Stance toward D Sitting Stance (facing D) Middle Punch with Right Fist Middle
