YUL-GOK 율곡

Chang-Hon Tul Purple Belt
Total Movements 38 Moves
Ready Stance Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Diagram Line Scholar Symbol 士 (Upper Line AB, Central Line EF, Column CD, Front Diagonals AD & BD)
Return Foot Left Foot

Pattern Meaning

Yul-Gok was the pen name of Korean philosopher and scholar Yi I (1536-1584), nicknamed the 'Confucius of Korea'. The 38 movements refer to his birthplace near the 38th parallel, and the diagram represents the scholar (士).

Key Technical Points & Memory Anchors

  • Part 1 (Moves 1-6): Extend fists in natural motion, followed by fast-motion punches in sitting stance.
  • Part 2 (Moves 7-14): Front-right & front-left diagonals: middle block, low front kick, and two fast-motion punches.
  • Part 3 (Moves 15-21): Marching palm hooking blocks and punches to D, pivoting with left foot into Move 15, including two connecting-motion pairs (16-17 & 19-20).
  • Part 4 (Moves 22-27): Bending stance left side kick & right front elbow strike (Kihap 24), then right side kick & left front elbow strike (Kihap 27). (Kihaps are school/instructor conventions).
  • Part 5 (Moves 28-38): Twin knife-hand blocks and fingertip thrusts, high side blocks facing back, jump left X-stance backfist (Kihap 36), finishing with double forearm blocks.
Part 1: Sitting Stance Natural Extension & Fast Motion Punches (Moves 1 - 6)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
1 D Move L foot to B Sitting Stance Extend Left Fist Horizontally (Natural Motion) Middle
2 D Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Sitting Stance Middle Punch with Right Fist Middle
3 D Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Sitting Stance Middle Punch with Left Fist Middle
4 D Bring L foot to R, Move R foot to A Sitting Stance Extend Right Fist Horizontally (Natural Motion) Middle
5 D Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Sitting Stance Middle Punch with Left Fist Middle
6 D Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Sitting Stance Middle Punch with Right Fist Middle
Part 2: Diagonals AD & BD - Middle Block, Front Kick & Fast Punches (Moves 7 - 14)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
7 AD Turn to Front-Right Diagonal AD (Move R foot) Right Walking Middle Inner Forearm Side Block (R) Middle
8 AD Kick to AD Kicking Position Low Front Snap Kick (L) Low
9 AD Lower L foot to AD & Punch (Fast Motion) Left Walking Middle Obverse Punch (L) Middle
10 AD Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Left Walking Middle Reverse Punch (R) Middle
11 BD Turn to Front-Left Diagonal BD (Move L foot) Left Walking Middle Inner Forearm Side Block (L) Middle
12 BD Kick to BD Kicking Position Low Front Snap Kick (R) Low
13 BD Lower R foot to BD & Punch (Fast Motion) Right Walking Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
14 BD Fast Motion (Maintain stance) Right Walking Middle Reverse Punch (L) Middle
Part 3: Palm Hooking Blocks & Connecting Motion Pairs to D (Moves 15 - 21)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
15 D Pivot L foot to face D, forming Right Walking Stance Right Walking Middle Palm Hooking Block (R) Middle
16 D Connecting Motion (Maintain stance) Right Walking Middle Palm Hooking Block (L) Middle
17 D Connecting Motion (Maintain stance) Right Walking Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
18 D Step forward (Move L foot to D) Left Walking Middle Palm Hooking Block (L) Middle
19 D Connecting Motion (Maintain stance) Left Walking Middle Palm Hooking Block (R) Middle
20 D Connecting Motion (Maintain stance) Left Walking Middle Obverse Punch (L) Middle
21 D Step forward (Move R foot to D) Right Walking Middle Obverse Punch (R) Middle
Part 4: Bending Stances, Side Kicks & Front Elbow Strikes (Moves 22 - 27)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
22 D Turn Face to D & balance on R leg Right Bending Ready Stance A Bending Balance Posture -
23 D Kick to D Kicking Position Middle Side Piercing Kick (L) Middle
24 D Lower L foot to D Left Walking Right Front Elbow Strike into Left Open Palm KIHAP! Middle
25 C Turn Face to C & balance on L leg Left Bending Ready Stance A Bending Balance Posture -
26 C Kick to C Kicking Position Middle Side Piercing Kick (R) Middle
27 C Lower R foot to C Right Walking Left Front Elbow Strike into Right Open Palm KIHAP! Middle
Part 5: Twin Knifehand, Spear Thrusts, Jump X-Stance & Finish (Moves 28 - 38)
# Dir Footwork & Action Stance Technique Target
28 E Move L foot to E Right L-Stance Twin Knife-Hand Block Middle
29 E Move R foot to E Right Walking Middle Straight Fingertip Thrust (R) Middle
30 F Move R foot to F (Turn 180° Clockwise) Left L-Stance Twin Knife-Hand Block Middle
31 F Move L foot to F Left Walking Middle Straight Fingertip Thrust (L) Middle
32 C Move L foot to C (Facing Back) Left Walking High Outer Forearm Side Block (L) High
33 C Maintain Stance Left Walking Middle Reverse Punch (R) Middle
34 C Step forward (Move R foot to C) Right Walking High Outer Forearm Side Block (R) High
35 C Maintain Stance Right Walking Middle Reverse Punch (L) Middle
36 C Jump to C Left X-Stance (facing B) High Backfist Side Strike (L) KIHAP! High
37 A Move R foot to A Right Walking High Double Forearm Block (R) High
38 B Bring R foot to L, Move L foot to B Left Walking High Double Forearm Block (L) High
