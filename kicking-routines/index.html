1. Kukkiwon Korean Kicking Terminology Crosswalk (차기 기술)

Standardized mapping according to official Kukkiwon technical terminology definitions:

Curriculum Name Kukkiwon Term Hangul Striking Surface Technical Execution & Biomechanics
Front Snap Kick Ap-chagi 앞차기 Apchuk (Ball of foot) / Baldeung (Instep) Knee chambers upward, snapping linear thrust striking with ball of foot or instep.
Roundhouse Kick Dollyeo-chagi 돌려차기 Baldeung (Instep) / Apchuk Rotational hip drive striking the flank, ribs, or temple with the instep.
Side Piercing Kick Yeop-chagi 옆차기 Balnal (Foot blade) / Dwichuk (Bottom heel) Linear thrust rotating supporting foot 180°, aligning hip and heel into target.
Back Thrust Kick Dwi-chagi 뒤차기 Dwichuk (Bottom heel) Linear rearward thrust looking over shoulder, driving heel straight back into abdomen.
Inside-to-Out Crescent Bakkat-chagi 바깥차기 Balnal (Outer foot blade) Arcing trajectory traveling from inside midline outward across the opponent's guard.
Outside-to-In Crescent An-chagi 안차기 Balnaldeung (Inside edge / Reverse foot blade) Arcing trajectory traveling from outside inward across the temple, neck, or chest.
Axe / Downward Kick Naeryeo-chagi 내려차기 Dwichuk (Heel) / Balbadak High vertical chamber descending forcefully downward onto clavicle or facial bridge.
Hook Kick Nakka-chagi 낚아차기 Dwichuk (Back heel) / Balbadak Extended linear chamber that flexes and rehooks the knee, pulling heel across target.
Spinning Hook / Heel Kick Dwi-huryeo-chagi 뒤후려차기 Dwichuk (Back heel) / Balbadak Full body rotation driving a wide whipping heel arc across the head (subtypes include 360° and 540°).
Whirlwind / Tornado Kick Category Dolgae-chagi 돌개차기 Baldeung (Instep) / Apchuk Full-or-more backward-turning rotational jumping kick category (encompassing 360° spin kicks and tornado kicks).
Repeated Same-Leg Kicks Geodeup-chagi 거듭차기 Specific to base kick Delivering two or more of the same kick successively with the same leg without setting foot down.
Alternating Successive Kicks Ieo-chagi 이어차기 Specific to base kick Continuous successive kicks of the same type delivered alternately with left and right legs.
Airborne Double Roundhouse Narae-chagi 나래차기 Baldeung (Instep) Rapid alternating double roundhouse kicks executed while airborne.
Sequential Jumping Front Kick Ttwieo-ieo-ap-chagi / Dubaldangseong 뛰어이어앞차기 / 두발당성차기 Apchuk (Ball of foot) Sequential airborne front kicks delivered alternately in mid-air.
Scissors / Split Kick (Simultaneous) Gawi-chagi 가위차기 Apchuk / Baldeung Simultaneous airborne split kick targeting two separate opponents or levels in opposite directions.
Jumping Kick Modifier Ttwieo-...-chagi 뛰어차기 - Kukkiwon jumping modifier (e.g., Ttwieo Yeop-chagi; Chang-Hon uses Twimyo Yop Chagi).

2. Belt-by-Belt Primary Kicking Progressions

White Belt

  1. Back leg front kick (Ap-chagi)
  2. Back leg round kick (Dollyeo-chagi)
  3. Front leg side kick (Yeop-chagi)

Yellow Belt

  1. Front kick, moving forward back leg round kick
  2. Jab punch, back leg side kick
  3. Moving forward spin side kick

Orange Belt

  1. Moving forward back leg front kick, double punch
  2. Backfist, sliding forward front leg hook kick (Nakka-chagi)
  3. Back leg round kick, side kick

Green Belt

  1. Circle block, step forward, back leg front kick
  2. Backfist, reverse punch, back leg round kick, step down, spin crescent kick
  3. Back leg inside-out crescent kick, same leg side kick without setting down, spin hook kick

Purple Belt

  1. Back leg axe kick (Naeryeo-chagi), spin side kick
  2. Back leg hook kick low, balance and same leg round kick high (Geodeup-chagi)
  3. Spin hook kick, side kick

Blue Belt

  1. Jab punch, reverse punch, back leg round kick, jump spin hook kick
  2. Front leg front kick, jump back leg round kick
  3. Back leg round kick, hook kick, side kick
  4. Crescent, crescent, jump side kick

Red Belt

  1. Backfist punch, back leg round kick landing in front, tornado kick (Dolgae-chagi)
  2. Spin hook kick, back leg round kick, jump spin crescent kick
  3. Front leg front kick, balancing high round kick, landing on left knee spin around and leg sweep

Brown Belt

  1. Front leg front kick, jump spin tornado round kick, back leg side kick landing forward
  2. Back leg low, middle, and high side kicks, set down in front
  3. Jump spin hook kick

Brown Belt Black Stripe

  1. Low knifehand guarding block, 360-degree jump side kick
  2. Jump front leg front kick, back leg round kick
  3. Jump back leg round kick and land in front

1st Dan Black Belt

  1. Low knifehand guarding block, 360-degree hook kick
  2. Jump split front kick / scissors kick
  3. Jump back leg round kick, 360-degree side kick

3. Belt-by-Belt Kicking Defenses (Counters to Incoming Kicks)

  • Against Front Kick (Red Belt): Outer forearm block, front leg front kick counter to groin.
  • Against Side Kick (Red Belt): Fall on left knee balancing with hands, sweep opponent's supporting leg.
  • Against Jump Spin Kick (Red Belt): Slide in close inside rotation, direct punch to face.
  • Against Axe Kick (Brown Belt): High X-block, trap/twist leg, drive opponent to ground.
  • Against Outside-In Crescent (Brown Belt): Step inside, grab behind opponent's neck, horizontal elbow strike to face.
  • Against Hook Kick (Brown Belt): Drop to ground, execute scissors leg takedown.
  • Against Side Kick (Brown-Black): Augmented block, reach hand behind neck, horizontal elbow strike to face.
  • Against Inside-Out Crescent (Brown-Black): Push-block kicking leg while evading sideways, counter with side kick.

4. Belt-by-Belt Board & Brick Breaking Requirements (Kyokpa)

  • Brown Belt: Jump spin hook kick; Palm heel strike through one concrete brick.
  • Brown Belt Black Stripe: 360-degree side kick through two wooden boards; Forefist punch through two wooden boards.
  • Black Belt: 360-degree spin hook kick; Hammerfist through one concrete brick.
  • 1st Dan Testing: Split front kick through two boards; Jump side kick / 360-degree kick; Jump scissors kick; Elbow strike through one concrete brick.
