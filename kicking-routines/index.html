1. Belt-by-Belt Kicking Progressions

White Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Slide back leg front kick
  2. Front leg front kick
  3. Back leg front kick
Yellow Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Slide back leg roundhouse kick
  2. Front leg roundhouse kick
  3. Back leg roundhouse kick
Orange Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Slide back leg side kick
  2. Front leg side kick
  3. Back leg side kick
Green Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Slide back leg hook kick
  2. Front leg hook kick
  3. Spin heel kick
Purple Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Jump front kick
  2. Jump side kick
  3. Jump roundhouse kick
Blue Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Jump spin crescent kick
  2. Jump spin side kick
  3. Jump spin hook kick
Red Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Hook kick, round kick, spin heel kick (same leg lead into opposite leg spin)
  2. Front leg roundhouse kick, tornado kick
  3. 360 jump side kick
Brown Belt Kicking Routine
  1. Hook kick, round kick, jump spin crescent kick
  2. Front leg roundhouse kick, jump spin side kick
  3. 360 jump crescent kick
Brown Belt Black Stripe Kicking Routine
  1. 360 push knee up round
  2. Hip buster jump hook
  3. Front kick then jump round
  4. Inside-out, outside-in
Black Belt Kicking Progressions
  1. Side kick, round kick, spin heel kick
  2. Inside-out crescent, outside-in crescent, jump spin side kick
  3. Front leg hook kick, round kick, jump 360 side kick

2. Kicking Defenses Against Incoming Kicks

Transcribed from Master Malone's primary notes under Red Belt and Brown Belt kicking defenses:

Red Belt Kicking Defenses
  1. Against Front Kick: Downward X-block, twist leg, back leg round kick.
  2. Against Side Kick: Open hand push block to knee/shin, jump spin side kick.
  3. Against Roundhouse Kick: Open hand brush block, tornado kick.
Brown Belt Kicking Defenses
  1. Against Hook Kick: Duck under kick, step in with double punch.
  2. Against Spin Heel Kick: Evade backward, counter with jump spin side kick.
  3. Against Axe Kick: Side step evade, grab opponent's leg and leg sweep takedown.

3. Board & Brick Breaking Requirements (Kyokpa)

Transcribed from Master Malone's curriculum notes:

  • Brown Belt: Palm-heel strike through one brick.
  • Brown Belt Black Stripe: 360-degree side kick through two boards; punch through two boards.
  • Black Belt: Hammer-fist through one brick.
  • 1st Dan Testing (Notes P0489–P0493): Elbow through one brick; split front kick through two boards; jump sidekick/360; jump scissors kick.

4. Official Kukkiwon Kicking Terminology Crosswalk

Standardized kicking terms referenced from Kukkiwon Taekwondo Technical Terminology Glossary (§3, pp. 11–21):

Technique Concept Official Kukkiwon Name Hangul Official English Gloss Chang-Hon / School Name Primary Striking Surface Technical Description
Front Snap Kick Ap-chagi 앞차기 Front Kick Front Snap Kick Apchuk (Ball of foot) Linear chamber and snap traveling forward into abdomen or chin.
Roundhouse Kick Dollyeo-chagi 돌려차기 Turning Kick Roundhouse / Turning Baldeung / Apchuk Rotational hip turn striking temple, neck, or ribs.
Side Kick Yeop-chagi 옆차기 Side Kick Side Piercing Kick Balnal (Foot blade) / Dwichuk Linear extension with hip rotation and supporting foot pivot.
Back Kick Dwi-chagi 뒤차기 Back Kick Back Thrust Kick Dwichuk (Heel) Straight rearward thrust looking over shoulder.
Hook Kick (Bent Knee) Nakka-chagi 낚아차기 Hook Kick Hook Kick Dwichuk (Heel) Knee extends and rehooks across the target.
Back Whip Kick Dwi-huryeo-chagi 뒤후려차기 Back Whip Kick Spinning Hook / Heel Dwichuk / Balbadak Full 360° rotational sweeping heel kick across the head.
Inward Crescent (Outside-to-In) An-chagi 안차기 Inward Kick Outside-to-In Crescent Balnaldeung (Inside foot blade) Arcing trajectory traveling from outside inward across target.
Outward Crescent (Inside-to-Out) Bakkat-chagi 바깥차기 Outward Kick Inside-to-Out Crescent Balnal (Outer foot blade) Arcing trajectory traveling from inside outward.
Axe / Downward Kick Naeryeo-chagi 내려차기 Downward Kick Axe Kick Dwichuk / Balbadak Elevated straight leg dropping downward onto clavicle or face.
Whirlwind / Tornado Category Dolgae-chagi 돌개차기 Whirlwind Kick Tornado / 360° Spin Baldeung / Apchuk / Dwichuk Full-or-more backward-turning rotational jumping kick category.
Sequential Jumping Front Ttwieo-ieo-ap-chagi / Dubaldangseong-chagi 뛰어이어앞차기 / 두발당성차기 Jumping Alternating Kick Jump Two-Foot Front Apchuk (Ball of foot) Sequential airborne front kicks delivered alternately in mid-air.
Scissors / Split Kick Gawi-chagi 가위차기 Scissors Kick Jump Scissors Kick Apchuk & Balnal Two simultaneous targets: one Biteureo-chagi (Twisting Kick) and one Yeop-chagi (Side Kick).
Double Roundhouse (Wing Kick) Narae-chagi 나래차기 Wing Kick Double Round Kick Baldeung / Apchuk Alternating airborne roundhouse kicks; first acts as a feint, second strikes.
