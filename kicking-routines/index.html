1. Kukkiwon Korean Kicking Terminology Crosswalk (차기 용어)

Standardized mapping from American curriculum names to official Kukkiwon technical terms:

Curriculum Name Kukkiwon Korean (Hangul) Romanized Term Technical Execution & Biomechanics
Front Snap Kick 앞차기 Ap-chagi Snap kick striking with the ball of foot (Ap-chuk) or instep.
Roundhouse Kick 돌려차기 Dollyeo-chagi Circular kick driving hip inward, striking with instep (Deung-bal) or ball of foot.
Side Piercing Kick 옆차기 Yeop-chagi Linear thrust rotating supporting heel 180°, striking with the foot blade (Dwit-chuk / Bal-nal).
Back Thrust Kick 뒤차기 Dwi-chagi Direct rearward linear thrust striking directly with the bottom heel (Dwit-kkumchi).
Inside-to-Outside Crescent 바깥차기 Bakkat-chagi Arcing kick travelling from midline to outside, striking with outer foot blade.
Outside-to-Inside Crescent 안차기 An-chagi Arcing kick travelling from outside to midline, striking with inner foot arch.
Axe / Downward Kick 내려차기 Naeryeo-chagi High vertical rise followed by a downward heel drop onto the face or collarbone.
Hook Kick 낚아차기 / 후려차기 Nakka-chagi / Huryeo-chagi Extended chamber that rehooks across the target, pulling with the heel.
Spinning Hook / Heel Kick 뒤후려차기 Dwi-huryeo-chagi 360° body rotation driving a whipping horizontal heel arc across the head.
Tornado / 360° Round Kick 돌개차기 Dolgae-chagi Jumping 360° spin transferring rotational momentum into a jumping roundhouse.
Repeated Same-Leg Kicks 거듭차기 Geodeup-chagi Two or more consecutive kicks delivered with the same leg without setting foot down.
Alternating Successive Kicks 이어차기 Ieo-chagi Continuous successive kicks delivered alternately with left and right legs.
Airborne Double Roundhouse 나래차기 Narae-chagi Rapid alternating double roundhouse kicks executed while airborne.
Jumping Kick (Modifier) 뛰어차기 Ttwieo-...-chagi Airborne execution modifier applied to any base kick (e.g., Ttwieo-Yeop-chagi).

2. Belt-by-Belt Practical Kicking Progressions

White & Yellow Belt (Foundations)

  1. Front Snap Kick (Ap-chagi)
  2. Roundhouse Kick (Dollyeo-chagi)
  3. Side Piercing Kick (Yeop-chagi)
  4. Axe / Downward Kick (Naeryeo-chagi)
  5. Step-behind Side Kick (Yeop-chagi)

Orange & Green Belt (Rotations & Heights)

  1. Inside-Out Crescent Kick (Bakkat-chagi)
  2. Outside-In Crescent Kick (An-chagi)
  3. Back Thrust Kick (Dwi-chagi)
  4. Repeated Low-High Roundhouse (Geodeup Dollyeo-chagi)
  5. Jumping Front Snap Kick (Ttwieo Ap-chagi)

Purple & Blue Belt (Linear & Arc Chains)

  1. Hook Kick into Roundhouse (Nakka-Dollyeo-chagi)
  2. Spinning Hook / Heel Kick (Dwi-huryeo-chagi)
  3. Jumping Side Piercing Kick (Ttwieo Yeop-chagi)
  4. Double Roundhouse Combination (Ieo Dollyeo-chagi)
  5. Tornado Kick (Dolgae-chagi)

Red Belt & 1st Dan Black Belt (Advanced & Aerials)

  1. Airborne Alternating Double Roundhouse (Narae-chagi)
  2. 360° Jumping Back Kick (Ttwieo Dwi-chagi)
  3. Spinning Hook Kick into Roundhouse
  4. Flying Side Piercing Kick (Twimyo Yeop-chagi)
  5. Jump Split Front Kick (Ttwieo Dubal Ap-chagi)
