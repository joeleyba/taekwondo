Form #1: Chon-Ji (천지)
19 Movements • Parallel Ready Stance (Narani Junbi Sogi)
Literally "Heaven and Earth", symbolizing the creation of the world and beginning of human history. Two similar parts: the first represents Heaven, the second Earth.

  2. Horse riding stance, push block, low, middle, high punch.
  3. Inner forearm block, back leg front kick.
  4. Grab wrist, back leg roundhouse kick.

Grab Defenses

Full Details →
  1. Same-Side Wrist Grab: Rotate wrist toward attacker's thumb, step back, and pull to hip.
  2. Cross Wrist Grab: Reach over with opposite hand, peel thumb, step back, and strike.
  3. Two-Hand on One Wrist: Form fist, grab fist with free hand, pull upward between attacker's thumbs.

Form #2: Dan-Gun (단군)
21 Movements • Parallel Ready Stance
Named after holy Dan-Gun, the legendary founder of Korea in 2333 B.C. Introduces knife-hand guarding blocks, twin forearm blocks, and high section punches.

One-Step Sparring

Full Details →
  1. Outer block, grab wrist, back leg front kick, elbow strike.
  2. "Pizza Man": Step forward to inside (horse riding stance), knife hand strike and knife hand block at the same time.
  3. Side kick, knife hand strike to neck.
  4. Arm up, step forward to inside (horse riding stance), reverse inner forearm block slapping chest at same time, left hand grab wrist, right hand knife hand strike to neck, reach behind head and pull forward, right knee to stomach.

Grab Defenses

Full Details →
  1. Two-Hand Front Lapel Grab: Double upward forearm strike between arms, followed by double palm heel strike to ribs.
  2. One-Hand Lapel Grab: Pin hand with opposite palm, rotate wrist into figure-four lock, step back into lock.
  3. Rear Wrist Grabs: Step back behind attacker's lead foot, rotate arm upward, and counter with back kick.

Full Details →

Green Belt

Form #4: Won-Hyo (28 Movements) →
Form #4: Won-Hyo (원효)
28 Movements • Closed Ready Stance A (Moa Junbi Sogi A)
Named after the noted monk Won-Hyo, who introduced Buddhism to the Silla Dynasty in 686 A.D. Introduces circular blocks, fixed stances, and bending ready stances.

Hand & Blocking

Full Details →
  1. Twin forearm block, sliding to cat stance, reverse knife hand strike, point, punch
  2. Moving forward circle block, reverse punch
  3. Knife hand guarding block, down block, reverse punch
  4. Circle block, same hand knife hand strike, step forward reverse knife hand strike to neck

Kicking

Full Details →
  1. Slide back leg hook kick
  2. Front leg hook kick
  3. Spin heel kick

One-Step Sparring

Full Details →
  1. Outside-in crescent kick, opposite leg spin heel kick (both sides).
  2. Outside-in crescent kick, same leg side kick without touching ground (both sides).
  3. Step forward to inside (horse riding stance), left arm outer forearm block and right elbow to temple, both hands grab behind head, headbutt, left leg round knee to ribs.
  4. Step forward to outside (horse riding stance), left arm outer forearm block, left hand reaches behind head, right elbow strike to temple, slide left hand down in front of shoulder and punch with hand, pull with left leg, takedown, right leg round kick on ground.
  5. Open hand by ear, step out to side, same time circle down punch block, opposite ridge hand to head.

Grab Defenses

Full Details →
  1. Front Two-Hand Choke: Bring right arm up, turn body 90° to the left, smash arm downward across attacker's forearms, follow with reverse punch.
  2. Rear Two-Hand Choke: Turn 180° raising lead arm over attacker's arms, elbow strike to ribs.
  3. Side Headlock: Reach over attacker's face, push nose/chin backward to break posture, strike groin.

Form #11: Koryo Poomsae (고려 품새) • Peong-Hwa (66 Movements)
Koryo: 30 Poom • Tongmilgi Ready Posture • Kukkiwon Yudanja
Koryo represents the seonbae (learned scholar of upright martial spirit) through Goguryeo, Balhae, and Goryeo. Floor pattern traces the Chinese character 士 (scholar). Three-Step Sparring (P0449-P0453)

Full Details →
  1. Punch 1: inner forearm back fist. Punch 2: low push block. Punch 3: circle block ridge hand.
  2. Punch 1: open hand brush block. Punch 2: open hand brush block. Punch 3: jump spin side kick.
  3. Punch 1: rising block punch. Punch 2: rising block kick to the groin. Punch 3: grab fist with both hands, twist and break arm over shoulder.
  4. Punch 1: push block punch. Punch 2: push block punch. Punch 3: push block move behind opponent and sleeper hold or snap neck.
