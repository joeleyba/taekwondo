Forms & Poomsae Suite
Form #1 White Belt
CHON-JI (천지)
Chon-Ji literally means 'Heaven and Earth'. In the Orient, it is interpreted as the creation of the world or the beginning of human history, performed by the beginner.
19 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #2 Yellow Belt
DAN-GUN (단군)
Named after the Holy Dan-Gun, the legendary founder of Korea in the year 2333 BC.
21 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #3 Orange Belt
DO-SAN (도산)
Do-San is the pseudonym of patriot Ahn Chang-Ho (1878-1938), who devoted his life to furthering education in Korea.
24 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #4 Green Belt
WON-HYO (원효)
Won-Hyo was the noted monk (617-686 AD) who introduced and popularized Pure Land Buddhism to the Silla Dynasty.
28 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #5 Purple Belt
YUL-GOK (율곡)
Yul-Gok is the pseudonym of philosopher Yi I (1536-1584), nicknamed the 'Confucius of Korea'.
38 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #6 Blue Belt
JOONG-GUN (중근)
Named after patriot An Joong-Gun who executed Itō Hirobumi, the first Resident-General of Korea.
32 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #7 Red Belt
TOI-GYE (퇴계)
Toi-Gye is the pen name of scholar Yi Hwang (16th century), authority on Neo-Confucianism.
37 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #8 Brown Belt
HWA-RANG (화랑)
Named after the Hwa-Rang youth group originating in the Silla Dynasty during King Jinheung's reign in the 6th century.
29 Movements Return: Right Foot
Form #9 Red/Black Belt
CHOONG-MOO (충무)
Named after Admiral Yi Sun-Sin of the Yi Dynasty, famous for deploying the armored turtle ship (Geobukseon) in 1592.
30 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #10 1st Dan Black Belt
KWANG-GAE (광개)
Named after Kwang-Gae-Toh-Wang, the 19th King of the Koguryo Dynasty who expanded Korean territory.
39 Movements Return: Left Foot
Form #11 1st Dan Black Belt
KORYO (고려)
Named after the Koryo Dynasty (918-1392 AD). Symbolizes 'Seonbae' (learned man of virtue and martial spirit). Kukkiwon 1st Dan Poomsae.
30 Movements Return: Left Foot
