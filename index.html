Chon-Ji

천지
Total Movements: 19 Moves
Pattern Meaning: Chon-Ji literally means 'Heaven and Earth'. In the Orient, it is interpreted as the creation of the world or the beginning of human history, performed by the beginner. Consists of two similar parts (Heaven & Earth).

율곡

Pattern Meaning: Yul-Gok was the pen name of Korean philosopher and scholar Yi I (1536-1584), nicknamed the 'Confucius of Korea'. The 38 movements refer to his birthplace near the 38th parallel, and the diagram represents the scholar (士).

06 Blue Belt

Joong-Gun

중근
Total Movements: 32 Moves
Pattern Meaning: Named after Korean independence activist An Jung-geun (commonly written Ahn Joong-Gun in Taekwon-Do), who assassinated Itō Hirobumi, Japan's first Resident-General of Korea in 1909. An was executed in 1910; the 32 movements represent his age under traditional Korean age counting (30 by modern reckoning).

퇴계

08 Brown Belt (2nd Kup)

Hwa-Rang

화랑
Total Movements: 29 Moves
Pattern Meaning: Hwa-Rang is named after an elite youth organization of Korea's Silla Kingdom. Members of the Hwa-Rang became influential military and political leaders. The 29 movements refer to the 29th Infantry Division where Taekwon-Do developed into maturity.

09 Brown Belt Black Stripe (1st Kup)

Choong-Moo

충무
Total Movements: 30 Moves
Pattern Meaning: Choong-Moo was the posthumous title of Admiral Yi Sun-sin of Korea's Joseon Dynasty. He redesigned, revived, and deployed the armored turtle ship (Geobukseon) during the Japanese invasions beginning in 1592. The pattern ends with a left-hand attack, traditionally symbolizing his death before he could demonstrate his full potential, constrained by his loyalty to the king.

10 1st Dan Black Belt • Chang-Hon Form

Kwang-Gae

광개
Total Movements: 39 Moves
Pattern Meaning: Kwang-Gae is named after Gwanggaeto the Great, the nineteenth king of Goguryeo. Beginning his reign in 391 AD, he expanded Goguryeo to its greatest territorial extent. The pattern has 39 movements, referring to the first two numbers of 391. Its plus-shaped diagram (+) represents the expansion and recovery of territory.

11 1st Dan Black Belt (Kukkiwon)

Koryo Poomsae

고려 품새
Total Movements: 30 Moves
Pattern Meaning: Koryo represents the seonbae: a learned person of upright character who combines scholarly discipline with a strong martial spirit. Kukkiwon connects this lineage through Goguryeo, Balhae, and Goryeo. The floor pattern traces 士, the character for scholar.
