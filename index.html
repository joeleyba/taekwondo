Forms Curriculum (Study Sequence) Chang-Hon Tul (1–10) & Kukkiwon Poomsae (11)
1. Chon-Ji 천지
White
19 Moves • Parallel Ready Stance • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Literally means "Heaven and Earth". Interpreted in the Orient as the creation of the world or the beginning of human history, performed by the beginner.
2. Dan-Gun 단군
Yellow
21 Moves • Parallel Ready Stance • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Named after Dangun Wanggeom, the legendary founder of Gojoseon according to Korean foundational tradition (dated to 2333 BCE).
3. Do-San 도산
Orange
24 Moves • Parallel Ready Stance • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Pen name of Korean patriot and educator Ahn Chang-Ho (1878–1938). The 24 movements represent his entire life devoted to furthering Korean education and independence.
4. Won-Hyo 원효
Green
28 Moves • Closed Ready Stance A • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Wonhyo was an influential Korean Buddhist monk and scholar who lived from 617 to 686 AD. He helped popularize Buddhism, including Pure Land teachings, in Silla.
5. Yul-Gok 율곡
Purple
38 Moves • Parallel Ready Stance • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Pen name of philosopher Yi I (1536–1584), nicknamed the 'Confucius of Korea'. The 38 movements refer to his birthplace near the 38th parallel; diagram represents the scholar (士).
6. Joong-Gun 중근
Blue
32 Moves • Closed Ready Stance B • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Named after Korean patriot An Jung-geun (1879–1910). The 32 movements represent his age under traditional Korean age counting when executed in 1910.
7. Toi-Gye 퇴계
Red
37 Moves • Closed Ready Stance B • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Pen name of Neo-Confucian scholar Yi Hwang (1501–1570). The 37 movements refer to his birthplace near the 37th parallel, and the diagram represents the scholar (士).
8. Hwa-Rang 화랑
Brown
29 Moves • Closed Ready Stance C • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Named after the Hwa-Rang youth organization of the Silla Kingdom. The 29 movements refer to the 29th Infantry Division where Taekwon-Do developed into maturity.
9. Choong-Moo 충무
Brown/Black
30 Moves • Parallel Ready Stance • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Posthumous title of Admiral Yi Sun-sin of the Joseon Dynasty, famed for deploying the armored turtle ship (Geobukseon) in 1592. Ends with a left-hand attack symbolizing his untimely death.
10. Kwang-Gae 광개
1st Dan
39 Moves • Heaven Hand Ready Stance • Lineage: Chang-Hon Tul

Named after Gwanggaeto the Great of Goguryeo, who ascended the throne in 391 AD and expanded Goguryeo territory. The 39 movements refer to 391, and the diagram traces the character 土 (Earth), representing the expansion and recovery of lost territory.
11. Koryo Poomsae 고려 품새
Kukkiwon
30 Poom • Tongmilgi Ready Posture • Lineage: Kukkiwon Yudanja

Represents the seonbae (learned scholar of upright martial spirit) through Goguryeo, Balhae, and Goryeo. Floor pattern traces the Chinese character 士 (scholar).
