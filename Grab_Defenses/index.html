Grab Defenses & Hapkido Self-Defense Guide

Wrist Releases, Chokes, Bear Hugs, Joint Locks, Weapons Defenses & Fatal Points

White Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
White Belt
Symbolizes purity and innocence in the beginning student.
  • Wrist grab (right and left): Pull away and back fist
  • Opponent grabs both wrists low: Clap upwards
  • Opponent grabs both wrists high: Clap upwards, twin knife strike to the neck
  • Straight across wrist grab: Turn wrist and push block with both hands, kick groin
Yellow Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Yellow Belt
Symbolizes the fertile soil from which plants will grow.
  • Cross wrist grab: Peel thumb, press wrist toward opponent
  • Cross wrist grab: Grab meaty portion of hand, twist and grab elbow with pressure
  • Diagonal wrist grab: Turn both wrists and push block with both hands
  • Opponent grabs wrist with both hands: Take free hand and reach through arms grabbing your fist and step back as you pull out (Hammer fist)
  • Diagonal wrist grab: Grab thumb, pull hand off and use other hand to also grab, circle foot around and take down opponent
  • Attacker lunges with strike or grab: Knife hand block and take down opponent with a behind the leg sweep
Orange Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Orange Belt
Represents the sunrise as the student begins to absorb technique.
  • Front choke hold: Grab left wrist, right arm up, step back with left leg, strike with right elbow
  • Rear choke hold: Step behind right foot with left while circling left arm around opponent's arms, punch stomach, elbow face
  • Opponent does a front choke: Step back and block elbow with your inner forearm
  • Opponent chokes from behind: Step sideways as you do an outer forearm block
  • Hand shake: Open up your hand and use your free hand to grab your fingers and step back
  • Hand shake: Use free hand to grab opponent's thumb and circle with opposite foot and pull attacker down
Green Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Green Belt
Symbolizes the green plant that has started and will continue to grow.
  • Attacker performs front bear hug over your arms: Take both hands and step and push on pelvic bones
  • Attacker performs front bear hug under your arms: Make phoenix knuckle and push into their ribs
  • Attacker performs a high bear hug: Drop to a horse riding stance, pray and lift up your hands (Hallelujah!)
  • Attacker performs a low bear hug: Strike hand, take heel and dig in on top of their foot
Purple Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Purple Belt
Represents the deepening sky as the student matures in technical execution.
  • Full Nelson: Peel hand off neck, twist, turn and palm strike face
  • Arm bar hold: Step back into attacker -> Elbow 1, 2, 3
  • Attacker grabs both wrists from behind: Step and raise arm up and cross it over the other and escape (Disco up, disco down)
Blue Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Blue Belt
Represents the sky to which the plant shall mature.
  • Attacker places you in headlock: Take front hand make a fist and push on outside of knee as you take other hand and push on the kidneys
  • Reverse armbar on neck: Turn into opponent, step forward and sweep opponent as you clothesline him with your arm
Red Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Red Belt
Represents danger, cautions the student to use control, and warns the opponent to stay away.
  • Front hair grab: Both hands -> Elbow to elbow -> Push down on elbow joint
  • Behind hair pull: Both hands -> Twist -> Elbow to elbow -> Push up to extend elbow
  • Front V-Neck Choke Grab: Weave through with arm -> Press hands together -> Push to side
Brown Belt - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Brown Belt
Represents stability and solid technical foundation prior to black belt rank.
  • Choke from behind: Raise left arm, spin pivot into a back stance and harness both arms with left arm, grab shoulder with free hand, bring opponent to ground with left leg sweep
  • Ridge hand strike: Push block with left hand, move forward to sweep with left leg, take down
Brown Belt Black Stripe - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Brown Belt Black Stripe
The final stage before black belt, demonstrating advanced weapons, breaking, and self-defense.
  • Knife attack to chest: Move to side away from opponent's lead leg, grab wrist and twist arm behind opponent's back
  • Knife attack to the head: X-block, grab opponent's wrist and twist and drive knife into opponent's body
  • Knife attack to midsection: Grab opponent's wrist, reach under armpit, grab opponent's shoulder and flip
  • Weapons Form: Nunchaku, Sword, or Staff (20-30 movements created by student)
Black Belt & 1st Dan - Grab & Self-Defense Techniques
Black Belt & 1st Dan
Symbolizes maturity and mastery in the student, unharmed by darkness.
  • Leg Bar Hapkido Defense: From right knee, palm heel strike right inside knee while sweeping ankle with left hand. Wrap right arm around lower calf and apply small circle wrist extension into Achilles tendon
  • Shoulder Throw: Opponent two hand front choke. Grab right arm with left hand. Swing right arm over left elbow, place inside of right elbow under armpit, squat low and throw over shoulder
  • Arm Lock against across punch: Move outside, hook right wrist around elbow joint, keep forearm trapped to chest and bow forward
  • Prone Position (On Back): Front choke wrist break & elbow press; Rear choke wrist twist; Stomping kick twin palm block and twist
  • Kneeling Position: Spear finger outer forearm block & ankle sweep; Downward knife hand X-fist grab and sweep
  • Chair Defenses: Lunge punch rising block into knee attack; Rear choke arm twist; Rear choke flip; Armbar hold thumb peel and elbow break
  • Nine Fatal Pressure Points: Pai poi, Chi men, Tang men, Navel door, Hui yin, Nao hu, Tien tu, Po lang, Ming men
